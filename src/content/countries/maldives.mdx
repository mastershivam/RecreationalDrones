This guide rests on excerpts of the CAA's drone guidance, a defence permit form and a consultation on its draft drone rules (MCAR-
UAS, issue 1.00) that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** Check the CAA for any change.

## The draft rules

The consultation response, as summarised, would follow the EU's approach: a drone is not registered, but its owner registers as an operator, gets a number and displays it on a sticker on every drone. Drones under 250 g without a sensor that can detect personal data, and toys, would be exempt. Specific-category flights would need a risk assessment and CAA approval.
