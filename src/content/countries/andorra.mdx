This guide rests on excerpts of the AAA's 2025 drone FAQ and the government's pages that a web search returned on 26 October 2026.
The texts are in Catalan and the translations are ours. **We did not open them ourselves.** The rules rest on the Air Navigation
Law of 9 November 2000 and a regulation on remotely piloted unmanned aircraft of 24 February 2016. Check the AAA for any change.
