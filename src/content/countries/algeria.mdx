This guide rests on excerpts of Official Journal texts hosted on the ANAC's site that a web search returned on 26 October 2026.
The texts are in French and the translations are ours. **We did not open them ourselves.** An appeals commission handles rejected
requests. Check the national centre for any change.

## Where you cannot fly

Overflying strategic and sensitive sites is prohibited. Making, buying, importing, selling or flying drones without the required approvals can lead to seizure of the drones and their payloads.
