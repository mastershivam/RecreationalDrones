This guide rests on excerpts of the DCA's drone FAQ, guidance material and MCAR-UAS that a web search returned on 26 October 2026.
**We did not open them ourselves.** Check the DCA for any change.

## Which rules apply

Mauritius regulates drones mainly through Regulations 91 to 91C of its Civil Aviation Regulations, with a newer framework, the Civil Aviation (Unmanned Aircraft System) Regulations 2024, layered on top: open and specific categories, remote pilot competency certificates and geographical zone limits. Which regime applies to a given flight is unclear to us. The 20 kg line separates a remotely piloted aircraft from a heavier one.
