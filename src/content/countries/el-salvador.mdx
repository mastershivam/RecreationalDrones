This guide follows the Civil Aviation Authority's (AAC) RPAS page, its May 2026 drone notice and RAC-RPAS edition 00 (19 December
2023). We read the RAC-RPAS PDF ourselves. The AAC's live page returned HTTP 403 to our fetch, so we read a web-archive copy of it
(11 February 2026) and its links. The text is in Spanish and the translations are ours. Check the AAC for any change.

## Flying as a recreational operator

A recreational drone flies in class A (open). Its largest dimension must be under 1 m, it must stay within 500 m of the ground
station and within visual line of sight (RAC-RPAS 5.010 a), b) and d)), no higher than 120 m (400 ft) above ground (5.010 e)), and
at least 30 m from anyone not taking part (5.010 f)). Under RAC-RPAS 4.005
the AAC bars flights at night, in cloud or outside visual conditions, over military sites, prisons, courts, government buildings,
embassies, hospitals, ports and other sites in the AIP, and two or more drones at once. It also bars flights from a moving vehicle.

Airports: under 4.005 no flight may come within 6 km of an international airport's perimeter, 2 km of an authorised private
airstrip, or 250 m of an authorised heliport. The AAC's RPAS page also has a map of operating areas around some airports. We have
not used it for the airport rule file.

## Not covered yet

- **Fees:** the regulation requires a fee receipt but does not give the amount.
- **Penalties:** the AAC can warn, fine, suspend or cancel under RAC-RPAS 2.045, which refers to articles 191 to 194 of the Civil
  Aviation Organic Law. We did not open that law.
- **Remote ID:** the regulation sets remote identification for class B only (RAC-RPAS 6.020 to 6.030). We found no class A rule.
- **Visitors:** RAC-RPAS 13.020 covers foreign operators, but we did not establish its full terms. The registration for a foreigner
  is valid for at most six months.
- **Whether the online form covers aircraft as well as operators:** we read the form's link on the AAC page, not the form.
- **Whether the AAC's May 2026 notice adds rules:** its text on the page is a pointer to RAC-RPAS and does not add rules.
- **Distances from airports and no-fly areas:** 4.005 gives 6 km, 2 km and 250 m. Whether these are the only limits is not clear
  from the regulation. We have not added them to the airport rule file.
