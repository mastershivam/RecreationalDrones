This guide follows the CAAC's real-name procedure AP-45-01R6, issued 25 June 2026. We read the Chinese text ourselves and translated
the parts we quote, so the wording here is our translation. It replaces the 2017 real-name procedure, which we read in English, and
it names the interim Regulations on the Flight Management of Unmanned Aircraft (无人驾驶航空器飞行管理暂行条例) as its legal basis. We did not read those
flight rules. The CAAC's registration system did not respond when we tried it. Check the CAAC for any change.

## What the 2026 procedure says

- **Scope:** section 1.4.3 requires real-name registration for civil drones flown in China. Drones flown only indoors are excluded.
  Self-powered toys and model aircraft are to be governed by a separate rule.
- **Owners:** section 4.2 lists Chinese citizens, Chinese legal entities and organisations, and foreign citizens and organisations.
- **Information:** section 4.3 asks for the owner's identity and contact details and the drone's maker, model, serial number and use.
  Makers file the model's details, including its empty weight and maximum take-off weight.
- **Validity:** section 4.6 says a registration lasts until it is cancelled, with a yearly confirmation.

## Not covered yet

- **The interim flight rules** for unmanned aircraft, so height limits, pilot rules, airspace, insurance and penalties.
- **Fees:** the clauses we read give none.
- **The registration portal:** its address did not respond, so we could not check whether registration is online only.
- **Toys and model aircraft:** the separate rule is not yet in the text we read.
- **Visitors:** the procedure covers foreign owners, but the ID they give and whether a visitor can register are not in the clauses we read.
- **Distances from airports and no-fly areas:** none found, so the map draws no circle.
