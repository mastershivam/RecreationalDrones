This guide follows Decree 34/2021 "Sobre las aeronaves no tripuladas", published in Official Gazette No. 70 of 23 June 2021
(GOC-2021-602-O70). The text is in Spanish and the translations are ours. We read the decree's text layer ourselves on
26 October 2026. The same issue also carries the Ministry of the Armed Forces procedure (Resolution 6/2021) and the
Transport Ministry's penalty procedure (Resolution 137/2021). We did not read those in full, so we cite the decree only.
Check the IACC for any change.

## Who may fly

Article 3 bans the use and manufacture of unmanned aircraft, and their import, for any use other than aeromodelling and
aerial work. Article 3.2 defines aeromodelling as recreational or sporting use "exclusivamente por los miembros del Club
de Aviación de Cuba" (only by members of the Club de Aviación de Cuba). Article 5 gives the Club administrative control of
aeromodelling drones. Article 7 defines an operator as a natural person over 18 accredited by the Aeronautical Authority,
for aerial work or aeromodelling.

## Aeromodelling

Article 12 says aeromodelling flights are requested by the Club's authorities from the joint flight-planning centres, and
fly only in approved flight zones. The Aeronautical Authority, with the Armed Forces, handles any flight outside those zones.

## Aerial work

Article 10 says people who need aerial work done contract GEOCUBA and its companies. Under article 11, GEOCUBA first asks
the Armed Forces units for authorisation, then asks the National Joint Flight Planning Centre for the specific flight by
12:00 the day before, for inclusion in the Daily Flight Plan. Article 8 lists the documents GEOCUBA must hold: a
registration certificate, an airworthiness certificate and a remote pilot licence.

## Restrictions and penalties

- **Restrictions for aerial work (article 13):** no flights over crowds, public acts or at night without authorisation; no
  flights in military zones within 8 km of airports or aerodromes; no photos, film or recordings without the permit set out;
  no flights with people, animals or dangerous goods aboard; and no flights that invade private life.
- **Restrictions for aeromodelling (article 14):** no flights over crowds, public acts, vehicle roads or recreational
  centres; no flights in abnormal weather or at night; and the same 8 km military-zone rule near airports.
- **Fines (article 26 and article 28):** 2,000 to 5,000 pesos, with confiscation of the aircraft for some breaches; licence
  suspension or cancellation for others. Repeat offenders can pay up to 50% more (article 27).

## Not covered yet

- **Fees, minimum age for a licence (the decree gives 18 for an operator), weight thresholds, height limits, Remote ID.** The decree does not set these out for individuals.
- **Insurance cover and amount.** The decree requires insurance but does not say what it covers.
- **Distances from airports and no-fly areas.** The decree gives 8 km from airports in military zones; it does not map them, so the map draws no circle.
- **Resolutions 6/2021, 67/2021, 137/2021 and 138/2021.** They appear in the same Gazette issue, but we did not read them in full.
