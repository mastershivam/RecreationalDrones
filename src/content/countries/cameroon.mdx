This guide follows the CCAA notice on remotely piloted aircraft, published on 20 November 2016, and the CCAA's drones page, which
we read on 26 October 2026. The notice is in French, and the translations are ours. The instruction it attaches, "Instruction 12",
is a PDF with no readable text layer on our machine, so we did not read it. The drones page holds only a menu link. Check the
CCAA for any change.

## What the notice says

- The Minister of Transport's press release of 22 September 2016 invited all operators of remotely piloted aircraft in Cameroon's
  airspace to suspend their activities and present themselves to the Direction Générale de l'Autorité Aéronautique for case-by-case
  review (our translation).
- The CCAA then published an instruction on how to apply for authorisation to use remotely piloted aircraft, for one-off aerial
  work. It says the instruction sets out what an application contains and how it is processed.
- The notice says a broader framework for drone operations was in preparation and would lead to regulation. We did not find that
  framework on the CCAA site.

The notice does not state fees, validity or the document list.
