This guide follows CAAS's unmanned aircraft pages, read on 25 October 2026. The pages are dated May to September 2026. The
CAAS recreational-rules leaflet is a PDF we could not read, and nor could we read the Air Navigation (101 Unmanned Aircraft
Operations) Regulations, so check them for the limits on height, distance from people and night flying that this guide lacks.

## Which rules apply

CAAS sorts flights by purpose: recreational, educational, and business or other. The requirements apply "to anyone flying a UA
in Singapore, including Singapore Citizens, Singapore Permanent Residents, and non-residents."

## Registration

CAAS says you must register an unmanned aircraft (UA) that weighs **more than 250 g**, except one used only for light shows
under a Class 1 Activity Permit. You buy a registration label at S$25 and complete the registration on the UA Portal. You must
be at least 16 to register one. Below 16, an eligible person such as a parent or guardian registers it. The pages we read
give no registration term. Deregister if the drone is lost or damaged beyond repair, the label is damaged or illegible, or you
sell or give the drone away.

Operating an unregistered UA above 250 g "is an offence. You may be fined up to S$10,000 and/or jailed for up to 6 months."

Above 250 g, CAAS also says to equip the drone with Broadcast Remote Identification.

## Pilot training

CAAS says you need a UA Basic Training Certificate or a UA Pilot Licence if your UA weighs more than 1.5 kg and up to 7 kg and
you fly it in a publicly accessible place or for an event attended by more than 50 people. Above 7 kg in those situations you
need a UA Pilot Licence.

## Where you need a permit

A **Class 2 Activity Permit** is needed for a recreational UA of up to 25 kg flown:

- higher than 200 ft (about 60 m) above mean sea level;
- in any restricted or danger area; or
- within 5 km of an airport or military airbase. (The requirements page says "airport or military airbase" and the permits
  page says "civil aerodrome or military airbase"; the pages do not reconcile them.)

An Operator Permit and a Class 1 Activity Permit are needed above 25 kg, for flying beyond visual line of sight, and for an
event attended by more than 50 people. You also need a permit from the Singapore Police Force to fly in a protected area.
CAAS's list of general no-fly zones is areas within 5 km of aerodromes, danger areas, prohibited areas, restricted areas and
protected areas under the Air Navigation Act. UA activities are not allowed in prohibited areas at all. Permit fees on the
pages: Class 2 Activity Permit S$110 per activity, Class 1 Activity Permit S$120.
