This guide rests on excerpts of the CAA's pages and its Regulation on unmanned aircraft that a web search returned on 26 October
2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** Check the CAA for any change.

## Where and how you can fly

The CAA's older, undated guidance lists a minimum 50 m from people and 100 m from security-sensitive objects, flights at most 500 m away horizontally and 100 m high within sight, and at least 8 km (5 miles) from commercial airports. We did not confirm these against the 2025 regulation. The CAA may set UAS geographical zones for safety, security, privacy or environmental reasons.
