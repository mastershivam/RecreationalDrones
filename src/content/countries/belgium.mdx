Belgium applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then links to Belgium's own authority for what is national.

<EasaRules />

## In Belgium

- **Register:** [Belgium's registration page](https://drones.mobilit.fgov.be/login).
- **Where you can fly:** [Belgium's map of geographical zones](https://map.droneguide.be/).
- **Authority:** [Belgian Civil Aviation Authority (FPS Mobility and Transport)](https://mobilit.belgium.be/).

## Not covered yet

- **Belgium's national rules.** We have not read Belgium's own drone rules, so what we say here is only what EASA says
  applies everywhere. Fees, how long registration lasts, the minimum age (a state may lower EASA's 16 to 12),
  insurance, penalties and any local bans are all unverified. Check the authority above before you fly.
- **Where flying is banned.** Belgium's geographical zones are on the map linked above, and we have not copied them.
