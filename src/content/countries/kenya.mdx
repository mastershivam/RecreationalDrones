This guide rests on excerpts of the KCAA's pages, its 2020 regulations, its implementing manual and the 2021 gazetted charges that a web search returned on
26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves**, and we did not check amendments after 2022. Check the KCAA for any change.

## Registration

Under the regulations an owner or operator registers an unmanned aircraft with the KCAA and gets a certificate. The manual says to apply online, with the
fee. Each aircraft carries an identification plate with its registration mark. Importing a drone or a component needs a KCAA permit, and the regulations
ask for security clearance first. A remote pilot licence is needed to act as remote pilot in command of a remotely piloted aircraft, for operations the
regulations specify (we could not tell which).
