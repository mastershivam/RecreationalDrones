This guide rests on the CAA's drone section on the Ministry of Transportation and Telecommunications site. We read it through a fetch summary, not the raw page, and we did not open the national portal page. The CAR006 regulation PDF returned a 403 from the site, so we did not read it, and we did not work around the block. Check the CAA before you travel with a drone.

## Current position

The CAA's drone section says all drone activities and related approvals in the Kingdom of Bahrain are suspended until further notice. It says this covers the suspension of drone operations and the processing of applications for permits or approvals. The section gives no start date. The only date on the page is a last-updated date of 15 October 2026, which covers the page as a whole.

## Rules we did not read

The earlier search excerpts gave a permit process, no-objection letters, a seven-working-day lead time and a form number. We could not confirm any of those on the authority's pages, so we have left them out.
