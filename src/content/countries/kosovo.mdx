This guide follows the Civil Aviation Authority of Kosovo's drone page, its FAQ and its regulations list, which we opened and read on 26 October 2026. The regulations list shows Regulation 01/2026 (published 23 January 2026, repealing the 2017 and 2021 texts). The official page for it, in Albanian, loaded only its title and metadata, so the title is our translation and its operative rules are unread. **The CAA's drone page and FAQ still describe the 2017 regime**, so the pages disagree with the 2026 act listing. We have not resolved which regime applies. Check the CAA for any change.
