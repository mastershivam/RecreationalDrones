This guide is based on two CAAB documents we read ourselves: the circular CAC-5 (Operating Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems, Issue 01, January 2019) and the draft Air Navigation Order (Parts 947 and 945, 2021). The live CAAB links failed our fetch tool and curl with a certificate error, so we read web.archive.org copies of both PDFs. We did not compare them with the live files. Both are in English. The guide does not cover the 2020 government policy "Drone Registration and Flying Regulation", which we did not open.

## Which rules apply

- **CAC-5** says anyone operating a drone needs CAAB permission before flying (an SFOC), must ask in writing 60 days before the operation, and must carry proof of liability insurance. Operations are limited to daylight in visual conditions, to 200 ft AGL, and to a point at least 10 nautical miles (18.52 km) from an aerodrome. The circular also bars flying within 5 nautical miles of a key installation, sensitive government building or built-up area, over forest fires, in restricted airspace, and over open-air assemblies of people.
- **The draft Air Navigation Order** is marked DRAFT. It sets categories (open, specific, certified), a minimum age of 18 for the specific category, and a flight authorisation from the Air Defence Operations Centre for any flight outside the Green Zone. Its fee schedule lists flight authorisation fees in BDT. We could not confirm whether any of this has taken effect.

## Where the two documents disagree

- **Height:** CAC-5 says 200 ft AGL. The draft says 100 ft.
- **Registration weight:** the draft gives 248 g in one place and 249 g in another, so we left the threshold out of the frontmatter.

## Not covered yet

- **Registration fee, minimum age for recreational flying, penalties, Remote ID and visitor rules.** The draft's Remote ID clause (945.42) is not clear enough to state.
- **Which document is in force.** CAC-5 is an issued circular, not marked as a draft, but we found nothing that says whether it is current. The draft says it is a draft.
- **Distances from airports:** CAC-5 gives 10 nautical miles from an aerodrome. The map does not draw that circle yet.
