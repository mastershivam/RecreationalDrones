This guide rests on excerpts of the GCAA's Part 28 directives (2018), its registration circular AC 28-008 (February 2023) and its
FAQ that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** Check the GCAA for any change.

## Where and how you can fly

As summarised: private and recreational flights stay within the pilot's sight unless the GCAA permits otherwise; flying above 400 ft AGL needs written approval. The FAQ sets a 10 km restriction around airports or helipads with published instrument procedures, and 5 km where none are published. The GCAA can allow a flight inside it on conditions. Light RPAS (1.5 to 7 kg) fly only in sight.
