This guide rests on excerpts of the GCAA's drone platform and news, and the Air Code, that a web search returned on 26 October
2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** A different site, airspace.gov.ge, says a licence is needed for flights beyond 500 m,
but it looks adapted from another country's text, so we did not use it. Check the GCAA for any change.
