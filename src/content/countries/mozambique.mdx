This guide follows the Civil Aviation Institute of Mozambique's operating directive DOS-09-2018 (dated 16 August 2018), which we
read ourselves from the IACM's PDF. The directive is in Portuguese and the translations are ours. It says it stays in force until
the IACM publishes its RPAS regulations. We did not check for a replacement. Check the IACM for any change.

## In Mozambique

- **Directive:** [IACM directive DOS-09-2018 on RPAS operations](https://www.iacm.gov.mz/app/uploads/2018/12/DOS-09-2018-Normas-de-Operacoes-de-Sistema-de-Aeronave-Pilotada-Remotamente-RPAS.pdf), the only source for this guide.
- **Authority:** [Civil Aviation Institute of Mozambique (IACM)](https://www.iacm.gov.mz/app/uploads/2018/12/DOS-09-2018-Normas-de-Operacoes-de-Sistema-de-Aeronave-Pilotada-Remotamente-RPAS.pdf).

## Categories

The directive sorts operations into Category A (personal and recreational) and Categories B, C1 and C2 (other operations). For
Category A, no RPAS may fly above 100 ft above ground at any time, and only in daytime VMC conditions (section 8.1 and Table 2).
For Categories B, C1 and C2, the maximum is 400 ft, and their pilots need an RPAS pilot licence or an ICAO state's certificate, a
class 4 medical and ICAO level 4 English (section 8.1).

## Registration and approval

- **Category A:** a notification letter to the IACM at least one week before the flight, and the IACM's written approval (sections
  8.1(i) and 8.2.2). The IACM says a non-resident's approval is limited to the time the passport or visa allows.
- **Categories B, C1 and C2:** a valid IACM registration certificate for the drone (section 8.3.1(b)), and approval of standard
  operating procedures and a risk assessment before each series of flights (sections 8.3.1(c) and (d)).

## Flying limits for recreational flights

The directive's Category A conditions (section 8.2.1) include daytime flight in VMC, no flights over people, at least 30 m
horizontally from people and property, flights only for personal or recreational use, and written permission from the landowner or
supervising authority to pass over private land.

## Not covered yet

- **Fees.** Category A needs proof of payment, but the directive does not give an amount that we could read.
- **Penalties, insurance and Remote ID.** None are in the directive text we read.
- **Minimum operator age.** The directive defines a toy by a child under 14, but sets no minimum age for an operator that we found.
