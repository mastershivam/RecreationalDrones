This guide follows the TTCAA's drone page, read on 26 October 2026. It refers to the Civil Aviation (No. 19) Unmanned Aircraft
Systems Regulations, which the page says Parliament passed in 2016. The page is undated and does not give a fee, a validity period
or a registration link. Check the TTCAA for any change.
