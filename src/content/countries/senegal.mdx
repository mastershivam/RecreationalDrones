This guide rests on excerpts of Annex 5 to the ANACIM's RAS 06 that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. The text is in
French and the translations are ours. **We did not open it ourselves**, and later changes are unknown. Check the ANACIM for any
change.

## Which rules apply

The annex sorts drones by mass into three classes: up to 5 kg, 5 to 25 kg, and over 25 kg.
