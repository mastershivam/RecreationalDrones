## Registration

The MTC says the DGAC has simplified registration: it is done in the MTC's virtual desk and the certificate is issued in minutes, where it
once took up to 30 working days. Law 30740 makes the DGAC's register of remotely piloted aircraft and their owners public and free. The MTC says
registering, accrediting the pilot and applying for an overflight permit cost nothing. Law 30740 exempts those who fly for sport or recreation from
the operator licence (articles 2.2 and 2.3).
