This guide follows the BCAA's drone page, read on 26 October 2026. The application form is the one the page links; we could not read its
content, only its title. The GIS Barbados notice on the online application returned HTTP 403 when we tried it, so we read an
archived copy of it, dated 11 February 2022. That notice gave the older contact details: 535-5318 and droneapplication@barbados.gov.bb.
The BCAA page now gives dronesinfo@pmo.gov.bb and 246-535-5318. Check the BCAA for any change.

## Registration

- **Who:** anyone who operates a drone in Barbados airspace, according to the BCAA page.
- **How:** the Prime Minister's Office's online application, before any flight.
- **Other requirements:** a training certificate for the device, a spectrum licence, and public liability insurance of at least
  $250,000.

## Where and how you can fly

- **Height:** keep the drone under 400 ft.
- **Locations:** aerial shots only at locations you have permission for. The page lists spots where a licensed and insured drone may
  fly freely for fun without special permission; we did not open that list.
