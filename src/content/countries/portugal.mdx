Portugal applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then sets out what ANAC, Portugal's authority, requires on its own pages, read on 26 October 2026.
Quotations from Portuguese pages are our translation.

<EasaRules />

## In Portugal

- **Register:** [ANAC's registration platform](https://uas.anac.pt/anac-registry/), with a digital mobile key. ANAC's
  support page says registration is free of charge.
- **Fee and term:** free of charge. The registration must be revalidated every three years (ANAC's operator page).
  Its page also contains a sentence saying five years, which conflicts with the three-year rule; we follow the three-year
  rule, which its FAQ and the rest of the page repeat.
- **Who registers:** a drone with a camera, microphone or other data-capturing sensor needs an operator, whatever its
  weight. Without a sensor, you register if the drone weighs more than 249 g, or could deliver more than 80 joules on impact
  (ANAC's FAQ, with its formula).
- **Visitors:** an EU resident registers in their own country. A visitor from outside the EU registers in Portugal as an
  individual if Portugal is their first EU country (ANAC FAQ). A foreign company must have a Portuguese address, a tax
  number (NIF) and an active CMD (ANAC's operator page, our translation).
- **Penalties:** flying without registration is an administrative offence (contraordenação) under Decree-Law 87/2021 (ANAC's
  FAQ). We did not find the fine amounts.
- **Where you can fly:** [ANAC's geographical zones map](https://dnt.anac.pt/mapa.html), linked from ANAC's platform.
- **Authority:** [National Civil Aviation Authority of Portugal (ANAC)](https://www.anac.pt/).

## Not covered yet

- **Minimum age.** ANAC's FAQ says a person registering on their own must be an adult with a digital mobile key and a main
  residence in Portugal. We have not found a minimum age for the operator, so the age field stays empty. EASA's 16 may be
  lowered by a state to 12, and we have not checked whether Portugal does.
- **Fines.** The amounts under Decree-Law 87/2021 were not found on the pages we read.
- **Insurance cover.** Portaria n.º 2/2021 requires a policy, but we have not read its minimum cover.
- **Where flying is banned.** ANAC's zones are on the map linked above, and we have not copied them. We did not read its
  restricted-zone pages.
- **Remote ID for open-category drones.** ANAC's pages we read set the Remote ID rule only for specific-category drones.
