This guide rests on excerpts of IDAC's drone guide that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. The pages are in Spanish and the
translations are ours. **We did not open them ourselves.** Older IDAC forum posts give a 2 kg threshold, which the current guide
appears to replace. Check IDAC for any change.
