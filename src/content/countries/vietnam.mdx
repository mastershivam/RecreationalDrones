This guide follows the CAAV's page on Circular 78/2026/TT-BCA, two CAAV news pages on drone registration and flight safety, and the
circular's text, all opened on 26 October 2026. The pages are in Vietnamese. The English is our translation, and the quotes and
article references come from a summary of the CAAV pages that we could not check word for word. The circular's PDF is linked from the
CAAV page and hosted by a third party. Of its ten pages, only the first two have a readable text layer, covering Articles 1 to 4. The
rest of the text, including Article 7 on registration, could not be read from the PDF. Check the CAAV for any change.

## Which rules apply

The circular details the People's Air Defence Law for drones and other flying vehicles, except those of the Ministry of National
Defence. It sets the registration system, the registration forms and the authorities that register. The circular's preamble cites
Decree 288/2025/NĐ-CP on unmanned aircraft and other flying vehicles. We did not read that decree.

## Registration

- **Who may register:** per the CAAV's summary of Article 7, a foreign individual may register if living in Vietnam, permanently or
  temporarily, or if a competent authority permits it. A foreign organisation needs Vietnamese authorities' permission to operate.
- **Where:** provincial police register private individuals' vehicles. The Cybersecurity and High-Tech Crime Prevention Department
  registers vehicles of diplomatic missions and vehicles assigned to local police. Commune police may register only under 2 kg, with
  the department's prior approval.
- **Fees and documents:** the summary gives no amounts and no document list.

## Flying

The CAAV's news page of 14 August 2026 says a UAV may operate only with a permit from the competent authority, within the permit's
area, time, scope and altitude. It says registration does not mean permission to fly. It says drones must not operate in areas that
threaten flight safety, and it names prohibited and restricted zones of airports and aerodromes, without boundaries or distances.

## Penalties

The CAAV page cites administrative fines, confiscation, and suspension of flying under Article 8 of Decree 282/2025/NĐ-CP. It gives no
fine amounts. For a drone that obstructs flights or seriously threatens aviation safety, it cites the offence of obstructing air traffic
under Article 278 of the Penal Code, with penalties from fines to up to 15 years' imprisonment.
