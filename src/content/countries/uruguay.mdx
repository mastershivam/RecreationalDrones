This guide follows DINACIA's pages, read on 26 October 2026. The pages are in Spanish and the translations are ours. Check DINACIA
for any change.

## In Uruguay

- **Register:** [DINACIA's Drone Registry](https://www.dinacia.gub.uy/servicio/registro-de-drones), free and online.
- **Where you can fly:** [DINACIA's page on the zones for recreational drone use](https://www.dinacia.gub.uy/documento/zonas-previstas-para-el-uso-recreativo-de-drones),
  which sets Resolution 093/2019 as the source.
- **Authority:** [DINACIA services](https://www.dinacia.gub.uy/servicios).

## Registration

- **Drone Registry:** DINACIA lists it as a public service for the general public, and the page says it is free ("¿Tiene costo?
  NO"). The page links to an online service at utm.dinacia.gub.uy. It does not say who must register or what a recreational flyer
  needs.
- **Operator permit:** DINACIA's news item of 28 December 2020 says that, from 31 March 2021, it will not authorise drone operations
  by people whose RPAS operator permit has expired, and asks operators to regularise. It cites Executive Decree 260/2018. The page
  does not say how to get or renew a permit, what it costs, or whether it applies to recreational flying.

## Where and how you can fly

- **Zones:** DINACIA's page says recreational drone use is planned for zones described in Resolution 093/2019 (its page is dated
  17 November 2021), at a height not exceeding 30 metres above the ground. The page refers to an attachment that lists the zones by
  GPS coordinates. We could not read that attachment, so we cannot list the zones.

## Draft rules under consultation

On 8 October 2026 DINACIA opened a consultation on draft aeronautical regulations for unmanned aircraft systems (UAS): RAU 100
(general requirements), RAU 101 (open category) and RAU 102 (specific category). The period is 30 calendar days. The page does not
mention recreational use, registration or height limits, and the drafts are not in force. The drafts are linked from
[DINACIA's documents page](https://www.dinacia.gub.uy/documento-a-futuro) and we did not open them.
