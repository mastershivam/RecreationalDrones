This guide rests on excerpts of the TCAA's drone pages and FAQ that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves.**
The TCAA's site also lists Civil Aviation (Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems) Regulations, 2026, whose text we did not check, so the requirements
may not match it. Check the TCAA for any change.

## Where you cannot fly

As summarised: near military installations, the State House, prisons, airports and airfields, government offices, national parks and crowded public places,
unless the TCAA gives special permission. Night flying needs a special permit. Flying without authorisation can bring confiscation, heavy fines and legal action.
