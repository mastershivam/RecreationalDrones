This guide rests on excerpts of the BHDCA's drone pages that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did not open them
ourselves.** Different copies of the page give different figures (a 500 m setback and a 120 m ceiling in the commercial section, a
30 m clearance, a 50 m distance from gatherings), and we do not know whether the 2020 rulebook has been amended. Check the BHDCA
for any change.

## Where you cannot fly

As summarised: commercial operators may not fly over groups of people or important buildings, and a flight within 1.5 km of an airport area needs the prior consent of air traffic control.
