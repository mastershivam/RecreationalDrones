This guide follows the State Civil Aviation Agency's drone page, read on 26 October 2026, and its registration instruction
(document SCAA-GAU-GM-01), which the page links as a PDF in Russian with an English text printed alongside. We quote the English
text where it is printed, and the rest is our translation. The Agency's page links three more documents that we did not open:
the airspace map instruction, the aviation rules AR KR-6 Part IV, and the registration form set. Check the Agency for any change.

## Registration

- **Who:** owners and operators of drones in Kyrgyzstan, including drones imported on a permanent or temporary basis.
- **Citizens and companies:** Kyrgyz citizens and Kyrgyz-registered legal entities and individual entrepreneurs.
- **Foreign persons:** a foreign legal entity or person may register a drone under temporary registration (Annex 2).
- **When:** within 10 working days of importing the drone or buying it in Kyrgyzstan.
- **How:** in person, by post or electronically, to the Agency. The Agency's own review takes up to 10 working days.
- **Contact:** +996 (312) 25 17 51, uav@caa.kg, 1 Azhybek Baatyr Street, Bishkek.

## Weight classes

The instruction sorts drones into classes by take-off mass:

| Class | Weight | What is needed |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| C0* | up to 150 g | No registration if there is no video or photo recording device |
| C0, C1, C2 | 151 g to 4 kg | Registration and an operator certificate |
| C3 | 4.001 kg to 25 kg | Registration, a remote pilot certificate and a technical condition report |
| C4 | over 25 kg | Registration, a remote pilot certificate and an airworthiness certificate |

The instruction's scope clause says it applies to drones of 151 g and above. The table says a drone of up to 150 g needs
registration if it has a camera. We read the table as the rule for lighter drones, and the scope clause as unclear.

## Marking

The instruction gives the minimum height of the operator's identification on the drone: 2 mm for 151 to 250 g, and 3 mm for
251 g to 4 kg, on the outer case. The label may be a weatherproof self-adhesive sticker.

## Changes

The owner tells the Agency within 10 calendar days of a change of owner, a modification affecting the drone's characteristics,
or the loss or destruction of the drone. Re-registration follows a change of owner or modification.
