Hungary applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA), and adds national
ones. This guide gives the EASA rules, then what Hungary adds. The Hungarian parts follow the Transport Authority's
page on registering drone systems and operators, the Aviation Act (1995. évi XCVII), Government Decree 4/1998 on the use
of Hungarian airspace and Decree 39/2001 on insurance, as published on net.jogtar.hu, and the FAQ of mydronespace, the official app run by HungaroControl, all read on 25 October 2026. These are in
Hungarian. Check the authority for any change.

## In Hungary

- **Register:** [the Transport Authority's page on registering drone systems and operators](https://kozlekedesihatosag.kormany.hu/hu/web/legugyi-felugyeleti-hatosagi-foosztaly/pil%C3%B3ta-n%C3%A9lk%C3%BCli-l%C3%A9gij%C3%A1rm%C5%B1-rendszerek-uas-nyilv%C3%A1ntart%C3%A1s%C3%A1val-kapcsolatos-%C3%BCgyint%C3%A9z%C3%A9s) (in Hungarian).
- **Where you can fly:** [the mydronespace airspace pages](https://mydronespace.hu/tudastar/legter) and app.
- **Authority:** [Transport Authority of Hungary](https://kozlekedesihatosag.kormany.hu/).

## Registration

Hungary registers **both the operator and each drone**. The Aviation Act says a drone, except a toy drone, may use
Hungarian airspace only if the aviation authority has registered it. The authority's page adds that the operator must
register before the first flight, and that a visitor from outside the EU registers in the first EU country where they
fly. A toy drone, in the Act's words, is a toy under the EU toy directive, under 120 g, with no recording device, and
unable to go more than 100 m from the pilot.

- **Fees:** HUF 2,000 for the operator and HUF 2,000 for each drone. A change of details is HUF 2,000, removal from the
  register HUF 1,000, and a certificate of registration HUF 4,090. Pay by bank transfer; a natural person may also pay
  by card or cash at the authority's office in Vecsés.
- **How:** paper forms, sent through e-Paper (with a Hungarian government login), by post, or in person at 2220 Vecsés,
  Lincoln út 1. We found no instant online registration. A minor (under 18) needs the legal representative's consent.
- **Term:** the page gives no end date. You must report changes within 15 days.
- **Marking:** show your operator ID and the drone's registration number on the drone, durably and legibly.
- **Visitors:** the Transport Authority page says a non-EU operator registers in the first EU country they fly in.
  mydronespace says anyone already registered in an EU state need not register again in another. Neither page says
  whether a visiting drone needs its own Hungarian entry; Hungarian law has no exemption for it other than the toy rule.

## Where and how you can fly

On top of the EASA limits, Hungarian law sets these:

- **Over homes:** a drone, except a toy, may fly over a populated area only in special airspace that the military
  aviation authority has designated for it on request. The Act defines a populated area as where an uninvolved person
  or persons live. mydronespace repeats this.
- **Check the map before each flight:** the remote pilot must mark the flight area on the official website or app
  before every take-off and keep checking during the flight that it is not banned. The decree says a fee may be set for
  the app; we did not find the price.
- **No Drone Zones:** flights are banned in prohibited airspace, in the No Drone Zones at Budapest (LHBP and the
  Budapest control zone), Szolnok, Pápa and Kecskemét, in restricted airspace unless its rules are met, and in
  dangerous and temporary restricted areas while they are active. Inside the Budapest control zone but outside the No
  Drone Zones, the limit is 40 m above the ground.
- **Aerodromes:** within the aerodrome traffic zone, only with the aerodrome operator's consent. Within 2 km of an
  aerodrome that has no such zone, and within 750 m of a rescue helicopter landing site, only with the operator's
  consent and a notice to the aviation authority at least 2 days before.
- **Protected sites:** the airspace within 300 m of listed national memorial sites and state-critical sites is
  banned. So is the airspace within 300 m of designated critical infrastructure, unless its operator flies or allows
  the flight.
- **Temporary limits:** the police and other bodies may restrict drones in an area for a time; the limit appears on the
  same website and app.
- **Special airspace:** for a flight that cannot follow these rules, you may ask for special airspace. The military
  aviation authority (the Defence Ministry's state aviation department) decides, according to mydronespace.

## Remote ID and insurance

- **Remote ID:** the authority says to show the operator ID on the drone. mydronespace adds that the registration number
  must also be loaded into the drone's direct remote identification system. A C1 or C2 drone may be flown only with remote
  ID and geo-awareness active.
- **Insurance:** required from 250 g, or for any paid work, and the authority says it must exist at registration. Decree
  39/2001 sets the minimum cover for a non-commercial drone: 0.25 to 4 kg, HUF 3,000,000 per event and HUF 6,000,000 per
  period; 4 kg to under 20 kg, HUF 5,000,000 and HUF 10,000,000. The authority says insurance is not compulsory under
  250 g, although the Aviation Act (section 69) makes liability cover a condition of operating any drone. We have not
  reconciled the two.

## Penalties

A breach of the drone rules can bring an aviation fine of **up to HUF 100,000,000** under the Aviation Act (section
66/A), imposed by the aviation authority or by the police. mydronespace adds that where a crime is committed with the
drone, criminal law applies. The operator is responsible for its drone's breaches; for an unregistered drone, the person flying it is.
