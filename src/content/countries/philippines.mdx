This guide follows CAAP's RPAS regulations page, its types-of-operations table and Parts 1 and 4 of the Philippine Civil Aviation
Regulations, read on 25 and 26 October 2026. None of the pages shows a date. The Part 11 PDF that would give the recreational rules
was unreadable. Check CAAP for any change.

## Which rules apply

CAAP separates commercial operations (for revenue) from non-commercial ones ("sports and recreational purposes"). Part 1 defines a
small RPA as under 7 kg and a large one as 7 kg or more. A 2026 analysis outside CAAP says the January 2026 Part 11 uses a 25 kg
open-category threshold and that the 7 kg figure is not in its text, so the two may conflict.

## Registration and permits

The table marks no drone registration, controller certificate or operator certificate for recreational drones, except at 7 kg
or above. Registered aircraft carry the marking "RP-U" and a number from 001 to 999 and a letter, on the electronic control module.
A **special flight permit** is needed for "flying at night, BVLOS, operating within 10km of ARP, flying over populated areas, and
operating above 400ft AGL". The page does not expand "ARP".
