Greece applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This guide gives
those rules, then Greece's own, from the Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority's (HCAA) drone portal, its registration page,
its FAQ and its visitor form, read on 26 October 2026. The Greek wording is quoted from a summary of each page, so our
English is our translation, and the Greek original is the reference. Check the HCAA for any change.

<EasaRules />

## In Greece

- **Register:** [the HCAA's UAS portal](https://uas.hcaa.gov.gr/Account/Login?ReturnUrl=/). This is the address the HCAA
  homepage links to. The address EASA gave, uas.hcaa.gr, did not connect when we tried it.
- **Where you can fly:** [Greece's map of geographical zones](https://dagr.hasp.gov.gr/el/#map_page). We have not read it.
- **Authority:** [Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (HCAA)](https://hcaa.gov.gr/).

### Registration

- **Who registers:** the HCAA portal says registration is open to people who reside in Greece. A resident registers once,
  however many drones they fly, and may not register in another EU state. An operator is the natural or legal person who
  owns, rents or intends to acquire the drone.
- **How:** online, on the HCAA portal. Registration needs a **fee stamp (παράβολο)**. The HCAA's registration page does
  not give its amount, and the fee document on the portal could not be read with our tools.
- **Age:** the HCAA's FAQ (Q11) says the minimum age for drone operators in the open and specific categories is 16. A
  minor operator aged 16 to 18 submits a parental consent declaration. We did not find what applies to anyone under 16.
- **Validity:** the operator registration lasts three years, after which it can be renewed. Remote pilot certificates
  are valid for five years (FAQ Q17).

### Visitors

- **Non-EU visitors:** the FAQ (Q25) says a visitor must also register in the first EU country where they intend to
  operate, and (Q40) that non-EU residents visiting Greece must register with the HCAA for the specific category.
- **The visitor form:** the HCAA's form at [hcaa.gov.gr](https://hcaa.gov.gr/el/form/application-uas-operation-in-geo)
  says it applies only to people who do **not** have a Greek tax number (AFM). It asks for name, nationality, permanent
  address, contact details and an optional passport scan.
- **The pages disagree.** The portal's registration page ties registration to residence in Greece, and the visitor form
  is keyed to having no Greek tax number, not to being outside the EU. We have not resolved which route a given visitor
  uses. Ask the HCAA before you register.

### Insurance

The HCAA's FAQ (Q12) says third-party civil liability insurance is required for each drone. Our reading of that answer
is a summary, and it says the rule applies to professional drones and to privately flown drones of 4 kg or more under
national law. We did not find a minimum sum.

### Where you cannot fly

We did not read Greece's own rules on zones, local bans or permission, so the EU rules above are all we give. The HCAA
portal links to a DAGR map (see In Greece above), which we did not read.

### Penalties

We did not find Greece's penalty amounts on the pages we read.
