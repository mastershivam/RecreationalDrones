This guide rests on excerpts of the RCAA's drone pages, FAQ and Part 27 that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did
not open them ourselves.** A 2025 notice of proposed rulemaking would add an operator certificate for commercial and international
operations; we do not know if it is final. Check the RCAA for any change.
