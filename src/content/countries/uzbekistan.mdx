This guide rests on lex.uz's pages for Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 287 of 31 August 2016, read on 26 October 2026, and
for its replacement, Resolution No. 658 of 15 November 2022, whose title we read there. The No. 287 page says the resolution
"lost force on 16.11.2022", and lex.uz says No. 658 superseded it. **We could not read the text of No. 658**: the cached page
gives its title and the Uzbek original's notice, but not the body. The lex.uz pages are the Uzbek legal database, not the
Civil Aviation Agency's own pages, and we did not reach the Agency. Check lex.uz and the Agency for the current rules.

## What the repealed resolution said

These points come from the No. 287 text, which is no longer in force. They show what the rules used to require, not what
applies now.

- **Admission:** civil drones were admitted to operate by the Civil Aviation Agency under the Ministry of Transport, after an
  application and a technical condition check. The 2016 text names a Uzbek legal entity as the applicant.
- **Registration mark:** five digits, painted on the airframe after entry in the register.
- **Radio equipment:** a separate permission from the competent body for the drone's radio equipment.
- **Airspace:** each flight needed a request, sent no later than 09.00 UTC the day before.
- **Fees:** no fee for considering the admission application, per the 2016 text.
