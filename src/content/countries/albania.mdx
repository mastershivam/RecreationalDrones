This guide follows the AAC's drone page and its operator registration page, both in Albanian, read on 26 October 2026. The
translations are ours. The drone page says it was last updated on 21 October 2026. Its rules rest on Orders 143 (18 July
2022, which transposes EU Regulation 2019/947) and 114 (6 June 2022, which transposes EU Regulation 2019/945). Check the AAC for
any change.

## Registration

- **Who:** only residents of Albania, and businesses with their main place of business there, can register. A person can
  register in one state only.
- **Where and how:** by the AAC's form, sent by email to dron@acaa.gov.al or by post to the AAC in Tirana. The form is
  ACAA-DFS-UASM-100 for a person and ACAA-DFS-UASM-129 for a company.
- **Fee and term:** the AAC charges a fee under Joint Instruction No. 11 of 24 December 2024. The amount is not on the pages
  we read. The operator ID and certificate are valid for three years from issue.
- **Age:** 16 is the minimum to operate, unless you fly under supervision.
- **Remote pilot:** a drone of 250 g or more needs the remote-pilot certificate (A1/A3). The AAC's page says the certification
  process has started.

## Where and how you can fly

- **Height:** at most 120 m above the ground.
- **Line of sight:** keep the drone in sight at all times.
- **Under 250 g:** the page says such a drone may fly up to 50 m from people, but not over crowds.
- **Airports and restricted zones:** do not fly near airports, heliports or areas the AAC has restricted, without special
  permission. The page gives no distance.
- **Crowds and privacy:** do not fly over crowds or gatherings, and respect other people's privacy.
- **A2:** flights need 30 m from uninvolved people, or 5 m in low-speed mode. The A2 certificate has an in-person theory test
  at the AAC.
- **A3:** flights need 150 m from residential, commercial, industrial and recreational areas.
- **Specific category:** flights beyond sight, with a drone over 25 kg, or over 4 kg above populated areas, need AAC
  authorisation with a risk assessment (SORA, or a standard scenario).
- **Certified category:** the AAC says it is still in development and not active for the public.
