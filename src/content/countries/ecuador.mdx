This guide rests on excerpts of the DGAC's drone pages and resolutions that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. The pages
are in Spanish and the translations are ours. **We did not open them ourselves.** The DGAC says RDAC 101 now regulates drones, and
a 2020 resolution with 2023 reforms also appears. Check the DGAC for any change.
