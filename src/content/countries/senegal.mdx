This guide follows Annex 5 to ANACIM's RAS 06 (Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems), second edition, March 2021, with
amendment 01 applicable from 20 April 2021. We read the text from an archive.org copy because the authority's own server
failed certificate checks for us. The text is in French; all translations are ours. Check ANACIM for any change.

## Which rules apply

The annex sorts drones into three classes by mass (2.1): class 1 up to 5 kg, class 2 over 5 kg up to 25 kg, class 3 over 25 kg.
Leisure drones (category A) can hold only a class 1 authorisation, which has a limited duration. Class 2 and class 3 drones
are not authorised for leisure flying (table in 2.1, and 4.2(b)).

## Where and how you can fly

- **Height:** at most 100 m (300 ft) above ground for categories A and B (4.4(a)). Higher needs ANACIM permission (4.3(a)(10)).
- **Night:** banned from 15 minutes after sunset to 15 minutes before sunrise, unless ANACIM authorises it (4.4(f)).
- **Line of sight:** the pilot must keep constant visual contact (3.3); a visual observer may help, up to two.
- **People:** no flying less than 50 m above a crowd without permission (4.3(a)(14)), and none over a town, village or built-up area
  without special authorisation (4.3(a)(18)).
- **Cameras:** infrared and other non-visible cameras and recorders are banned on categories A and B (4.3(b)).
- **Aerodromes:** no flying near one without formal authorisation (3.9, 4.3(a)(8)). The restricted zone is a radius around the
  aerodrome's reference point: 1.5 km if the runway is under 1,200 m, 3 km if it is 1,200 to 2,000 m, and 10 km if it is over
  2,000 m. Those figures are a candidate for the airport-rules file, since the zone depends on runway length.
- **Restricted areas:** no flying in a special-status airspace published in Senegal's AIP without the competent authority's
  permission (3.8(a)), nor over military installations without agreement (3.8(b)).

## Not covered yet

- **Fees:** the annex refers to the aeronautical fee decree (Decree 2019-1120) but gives no amount, and we did not open the decree.
- **Penalties:** section 7.11 says breaches bring administrative and financial penalties, but gives no figures.
- **Remote ID:** we found no rule for remote identification.
