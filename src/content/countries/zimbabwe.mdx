This guide rests on excerpts of CAAZ's drone registration and safety pages and FAQ that a web search returned on 26 October 2026.
**We did not open them ourselves.** The FAQ pages may not be current. Check CAAZ for any change.

## Where you cannot fly

As summarised: drones are prohibited within 5.5 km (3 nautical miles) of any airport or airfield. Flying in restricted or prohibited airspace needs an ROC and CAAZ's approval.
