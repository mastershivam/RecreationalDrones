This guide rests on three SHGM sources: the İHA Takip home page (read from an archived copy), its FAQ (read live through a fetch summary), and the SHT-İHA Rev.04 instruction (2020, read from an archived copy). Our translations are marked as ours in the text. The live SHGM server did not load from our connection, and the 2026 instruction PDF is not in the archive, so **the 2026 rules are unverified**. The FAQ and the Rev.04 text disagree on pilot age (see below). Check SHGM for any change.

## Registration

SHGM's home page says drones of 500 g or more must be registered in the online system, and that owners and pilots must register too. Under the Rev.04 text, an owner gives the drone's details, the owner's ID number and contact details, and a criminal record certificate (art. 11(3)). Sellers register a drone the same day it is sold. A drone brought in from abroad, or transferred within Türkiye, must be registered within three days (art. 11(2)). Each registered drone gets a label or code (art. 11(5)).

The FAQ says foreigners can register if they have Turkish citizenship, a residence permit in Türkiye, or a company there (FAQ 24). It does not cover short-term visitors.

## Where and how you can fly

For drones of 500 g to 25 kg (classes İHA0 and İHA1), the Rev.04 text allows sport and hobby flights only in green (free) zones (art. 19(5)). Green zones are published in Türkiye's AIP (ENR 5.5). Flights in green zones get permission automatically through the system (art. 17(1)(a)). Areas that need permission require an application 5 working days ahead, and red zones require 10 working days (art. 17(1)(b) and (c)).

Red zones include anything closer than 5 NM (9 km) to the nearest runway edge of an airport, areas around navigation aids and heliports, areas above 400 ft, prohibited and restricted areas, the vicinity of military sites and prisons, and areas published by NOTAM (art. 18(1)). Flying there needs a risk analysis and SHGM's permission.

Conditions for green-zone flights include daylight, at least 2 km visibility, the drone in sight within 500 m horizontally, no more than 400 ft (120 m) above ground, and at least 50 m from people and structures (art. 19(1)).

## Pilot age and training

The FAQ says persons who use a drone must be 18 (FAQ 12). The Rev.04 text says İHA0 pilots must be at least 12 and İHA1 pilots at least 15 (art. 14(3)). We could not resolve this, so we have listed 18, the FAQ's figure. Hobby flying needs no training (art. 14(4)).

## Insurance and penalties

Commercial flights need third-party liability insurance (FAQ 46). The Rev.04 text ties the insurance duty to drones over 25 kg and to commercial flights (art. 10(2)). The FAQ's minimum limits are in the field above.

The FAQ lists administrative fines, prison sentences, licence revocation and seizure of the drone as possible penalties (FAQ 67). It gives no amounts. The Rev.04 text says breaches of the instruction are subject to criminal sanctions under SHY-İPC (art. 27), and the İHA Takip insurance page refers to the penalty provisions of Part 5 of Law No. 2920. We found no penalty amounts in the texts we read.

- **Visitors:** the FAQ covers foreigners who live in Türkiye. Rev.04 art. 17(5) sets a route for foreign operators: a 20-working-day application through diplomatic channels, with a Turkish pilot supervising. We have not resolved whether that applies to a visiting hobby flyer.
- **Airport distance:** the 9 km red zone is in the Rev.04 text (art. 18(1)(a)). See the note to the lead on the airport rule.
