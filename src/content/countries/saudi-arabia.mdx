This guide rests on excerpts of GACA's portal and of GACAR Parts 48 and 101 that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did not open the
documents ourselves.** Part 101 also refers to older registration rules under Part 47. Check GACA for any change.

## Registration and permits

GACA's technical guidance says there is no difference between registering a recreational and a commercial drone; both go under Part 48. Part 101 says
no one may operate a model aircraft without a GACA registration permit and registration marks, and an operator permit, with eligibility that includes being
16 or older. Club authorisation does not exempt members from registering. GACA's FAQ says changing a drone from recreational to commercial use means
deregistering and registering it again with a freelance or commercial document and the fees.
