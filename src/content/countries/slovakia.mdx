Slovakia applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then links to Slovakia's own authority for what is national.

<EasaRules />

## In Slovakia

- **Register:** with Transport Authority of the Slovak Republic ([its website](https://www.dopravnyurad.sk/)).
- **Where you can fly:** [Slovakia's map of geographical zones](https://gis.lps.sk/vfrm/index.html).
- **Authority:** [Transport Authority of the Slovak Republic](https://www.dopravnyurad.sk/).
- **Note:** EASA's list links to a registration page for Slovakia that did not load when we checked, so we give no registration link.

## Not covered yet

- **Slovakia's national rules.** We have not read Slovakia's own drone rules, so what we say here is only what EASA says
  applies everywhere. Fees, how long registration lasts, the minimum age (a state may lower EASA's 16 to 12),
  insurance, penalties and any local bans are all unverified. Check the authority above before you fly.
- **Where flying is banned.** Slovakia's geographical zones are on the map linked above, and we have not copied them.
