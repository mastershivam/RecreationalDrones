This guide rests on excerpts of the CAAV's pages and Circular 78/2026/TT-BCA that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. The
pages are in Vietnamese and the translations are ours. **We did not open them ourselves.** Decree 222/2026/NĐ-CP on flight
activities (in force from 1 July 2026) also applies. Check the CAAV for any change.
