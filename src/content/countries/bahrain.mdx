This guide rests on excerpts of the CAA's pages and the Bahrain national portal that a web search returned on 26 October 2026.
**We did not open them ourselves.** One of the two pages carries an age tag that does not fit a March 2026 suspension, and the
permit rules come from older-looking pages. Check the CAA before you travel with a drone.
