This guide follows the GCAA's drones page (read 26 October 2026), Civil Aviation Regulations Part XVI (undated in the copy we read)
and the GCAA directive GCAA/ASR/DIR/2017-01 R1 (revised 25 September 2020, effective 8 September 2020). We read all three ourselves.
The GCAA's own guidance and its regulation disagree on several points, listed under "Not covered yet". Check the GCAA for any change.

## Where the sources disagree

- **Weight for the recreational exemption:** Part XVI exempts only micro and very small drones (2 kg and under), and only under
  three conditions. The GCAA page and the 2020 directive exempt a recreational drone of 7 kg or less from the permit. The
  directive does not mention registration.
- **Height and distance:** Part XVI caps micro and very small drones at 30 m, and small drones at 120 m. The directive (and the
  GCAA page that repeats it) allows up to 150 m above the terrain and 500 m from the pilot, and bars flights over congested areas
  without approval.
- **Aerodrome distance:** Part XVI bars micro and very small drones within 2 km of an aerodrome boundary, and small drones within
  5 km. The directive bars flights in an aerodrome traffic zone without the Authority's and air traffic control's permission.

## Not covered yet

- **Whether a visitor can register or fly:** Part XVI limits registration to citizens, CARICOM nationals, permanent residents and
  Guyana or CARICOM companies. Regulation 5(5) lets the Authority allow a drone registered abroad to fly here. We could not say
  how a visitor should apply.
- **Fees:** the regulation requires a fee but does not give the amount.
- **Minimum age:** Part XVI sets 18 for a commercial or experimental operator certificate. It sets no age for recreational flying.
- **Penalties:** Part XVI regulation 26 allows the Authority to suspend or revoke approvals. The directive refers to prosecution under
  other Guyanese laws. We did not open those laws.
- **Insurance for recreational flying, Remote ID and the 2022 draft:** the pages we read do not cover them, and we did not open the
  2022 draft.
- **Whether the directive or Part XVI prevails:** we cannot tell from the texts.
- **Distances from airports and no-fly areas:** Part XVI regulation 21 gives distances. We have not added them to the airport rule file.
