This guide follows the Monaco government's page "How to apply for approval to operate drones" (last updated 20 March 2023), which we opened and read on 26 October 2026. The page cites Sovereign Ordinance 5.266 (26 March 2015), Ministerial Order 2021-532 (2 August 2021) and Ministerial Order 2021-788 (13 December 2021). We did not open the legal texts themselves. Check the Civil Aviation Directorate for any change.

## Where you cannot fly

Without special authorisation, the page says you may not fly:

- above the Place du Palais and the Prince's Palace;
- less than 150 m from the boundaries of the Monaco heliport. This is a heliport boundary, not an airport radius;
- above the whole Principality during periods set by Ministerial Decree.

Model aircraft under 100 g are freely permitted, subject to the same location restrictions.
