This guide rests on excerpts of the CAAI's recreational-licence study material that a web search returned on 26 October 2026.
**gov.il refused our requests**, so we could not open the study guide or the regulations. The guide describes itself as general
information, not legal advice. Read the regulations on gov.il before you rely on this. Check the CAAI for any change.

## Licence and registration

The study guide says an applicant for the recreational licence must be at least 12 and pass a knowledge test, and that the CAAI
can refuse a licence for up to a year if the applicant gave false information or is likely to endanger people or property.
Under the 2024 regulations every drone except a "small drone" is registered; a registration lasts four years and can be
renewed for four years at a time. Owners tell the CAAI within 14 days if a drone is destroyed, stolen or sold.

## Where and how you can fly

The study guide gives a maximum height of 50 m above the surface, no night flying for recreational pilots without a commercial
licence, and limits on flying over people and infrastructure.
