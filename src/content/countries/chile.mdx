This guide rests on excerpts of the DGAC's pages and its DAN 151 documents that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. The documents are
in Spanish and the translations are ours. **We could not open the pages or the PDFs themselves** (the DGAC site returned only its title to our
fetch tool), so read them before you rely on this. Check the DGAC for any change.

## Which rules apply

DAN 151 covers remotely piloted aircraft (RPAS) of up to 9 kg of take-off weight. Operation away from populated areas also falls under DAN 91, and
secondary sources say it needs no credential or registration there, but still the DGAC's authorisation. We could not confirm that on a DGAC page.

## Registration, credential and authorisation

To fly, DAN 151 (edition 3) as summarised asks for the registration card, the pilot's credential and the DGAC's authorisation, direct sight of the
aircraft at all times, and third-party insurance. The file name of edition 4 suggests a draft for consultation, so we cannot say if it applies yet.
It renames the credential "Credencial de Operador de RPAS" and excepts aircraft of up to 250 g flown at up to 100 ft.
