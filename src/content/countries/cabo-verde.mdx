This guide follows the AAC's drone page, dated 19 February 2018 on the page, which we read in full on 26 October 2026 from the AAC
site. The page is in Portuguese, and the translations are ours. We did not open the AAC's legislation or the form the page links,
so any registration or test rule is not established. Check the AAC for any change.

## Where you can fly

- **Aerodromes and routes:** no drone operation within 2,000 m of any aerodrome or landing site, or on aircraft flight routes.
- **Height:** above 120 m (400 ft) above the surface, only with the AAC's written authorisation, and under its conditions.
- **No authorisation needed** on these conditions together: outside restricted areas; at or below 120 m; the operator within 100 m
  and in direct control of the drone; and good visibility.
- **Conduct:** the operator is responsible for the flight, keeps clear of aircraft and other property, and gives way to aircraft.
  The pilot keeps the drone in sight with its identification lights on, and follows the manufacturer's instructions.

## Authorisation

Above 120 m, or outside the conditions above, the AAC requires a request on its aeronautical servitude form. The page says to send
it so that it reaches the AAC at least 10 working days before the operation. The page does not give fees or a validity period.
