This guide rests on excerpts of the CAA's CAR-102 (revised December 2021, one copy marked as a draft) and its newer drone
regulation that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** Check the CAA for any change.

## Where you need approval

As summarised: flights within 5.5 km (3 nautical miles) of an air-traffic-controlled aerodrome need prior CAA approval, and drones are generally not allowed in controlled airspace without it. The newer regulation excepts toy drones of 250 g or less that are limited to 100 m and carry no sensors or cameras.
