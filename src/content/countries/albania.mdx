This guide rests on excerpts of the AAC's drone page that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. The page is in Albanian and
the translations are ours. **We did not open it ourselves.** A draft amendment (notice dated 17 March 2026) would align the orders
with EU Regulation 2024/1110. Check the AAC for any change.

## Which rules apply

Orders 143 (18 July 2022, the EU's operating rules) and 114 (6 June 2022, the EU's rules on drones and their operators) apply to every drone, whatever its use, size or weight. Specific-category operations need prior AAC authorisation, with a risk assessment.
