This guide follows CAAF's drone page and form OP 137 (Rev. 220321), which we opened and read on 26 October 2026. The OP 137 form quotes
regulations 78 and 78A of the Fiji Air Navigation Regulations; we read that quotation, not the regulations themselves. The
[online application page](https://www.caaf.org.fj/droneapplications) is a Smartsheet embed, so we did not see the form's fields
on screen. The commercial form OP 138 and CAAF's FAQ answers were linked but not read. Check CAAF for any change.

## Where and how you can fly

For recreational flying, CAAF's drone page and form OP 137 give these limits:

- **Height:** do not fly above 200 ft (about 61 m) unless CAAF permits more.
- **Distance from aerodromes:** do not fly within 3 km of a domestic aerodrome reference point or 5 km of an international one, unless CAAF approves it.
- **Line of sight:** keep the drone in sight at all times, unless CAAF has approved otherwise.
- **Small drones:** under 2 kg, keep at least 30 m from people, vehicles and buildings.
- **Places to avoid:** airstrips, helipads, fuel depots, government and sensitive sites (police stations, military barracks, prisons, hospitals, Parliament), national parks and conservation areas, and public gatherings, sporting events and populous areas. Private property and resorts need the owner's or management's permission.

## Not covered yet

- **Fees for recreational flying.** The pages we read give none for OP 137. The drone page says commercial fees apply "from $365.63", with no period given.
- **How long an OP 137 registration lasts.** The form and the page do not say.
- **Minimum age, penalties, Remote ID, and insurance for recreation.** The drone page asks for liability insurance only for commercial work.
- **Whether a recreational pilot needs a licence.** CAAF's RPAS FAQ page is linked, but its answers did not come through in the HTML we fetched.
- **Controlled airspace.** The drone page says to contact CAAF about local no-fly zones; the answer on controlled airspace was not read.
- **Visitors.** Form OP 137 asks for a passport, a tourist or resident status, and a duration of stay, so visitors seem to apply, but no page says so outright.
- **Contradictions.** The drone page calls OP 137 "online", while the form PDF says to email or post it. The form's "permit" wording (see the reason above) is not explained on any page we read. A form called GS900 that an earlier search mentioned did not appear on the pages we read.
- **The 5 km circle on our map** is drawn from the centre of every airport in OurAirports, domestic or international. CAAF's rule is 3 km for a domestic aerodrome and 5 km for an international one, so the map is wider than the rule at domestic airports.
