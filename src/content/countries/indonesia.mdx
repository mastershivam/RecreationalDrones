This guide rests on excerpts of the Ministry's pages, an undated slide deck on its SIDOPI system and PM 63/2021 that a web search returned on 26 October
2026. The pages are in Indonesian and the translations are ours. **We did not open them ourselves.** On 26 October 2026 the Ministry's live page
returned a "Request Rejected" block, the PM 63/2021 PDF and the slide deck could not be downloaded, and their web archive copies had no content.
The only page we read was a Wayback Machine copy of the Ministry's drone page, which is a 2015 news item about PM 90/2015. Check the Ministry for any change.

## Which rules apply

PM 63/2021 is the Civil Aviation Safety Regulation Part 107 on small unmanned aircraft systems, enacted 8 July 2021, covering operation, remote pilot
certification and registration. The Ministry lists PM 63/2021, PM 34/2021 and PM 37/2020 as the rules on drones.
