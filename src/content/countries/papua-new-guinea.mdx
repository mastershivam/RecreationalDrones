This guide follows CASA PNG's rules as we read them on 26 October 2026: Part 102 (applicable 19 November 2025), Part 47 (3 November 2022)
and Part 101 (2 November 2021). Part 101 no longer covers remotely piloted aircraft, so the older 2020 Part 101 copy is out of date.
Check CASA PNG for later amendments.

## Where and how you can fly

Under Part 102's standard operating conditions (rule 102.55), a remotely piloted aircraft is flown within these limits:

- visual line of sight at all times, below the cloud base (rule 102.85);
- at or below 400 ft (120 m) above ground level, in daylight;
- at least 30 m (100 ft) horizontally from anyone not directly involved;
- not in a prohibited or restricted area under Part 71, not over a populated area or an assembly of people, and not in a control zone;
- not within 3 nm, or 5 km, of a controlled aerodrome's reference point.

Within 3 nm of an uncontrolled aerodrome's reference point, the operator needs an agreement with the aerodrome operator, an observer for each pilot, and a height limit of 400 ft unless the Director approves more (rule 102.75). Within 3 nm of a controlled aerodrome, the operator needs an authorisation from the air traffic control unit. Open-category flying of a drone up to 10 kg (rule 102.95) needs no pilot qualification more than 3 nm from an aerodrome (rule 102.103).

## Not covered yet

- **The fee for form CA 47/01, how it is submitted, and whether registration is online.** The rules say only "with a payment of the appropriate application fee prescribed by regulations".
- **The pilot qualification** needed within 3 nm of an aerodrome (rules 102.75 and 102.103), and how to get one.
- **Minimum flying age, penalties, Remote ID, and insurance.** Rule 47.105 requires a registered remotely piloted aircraft to carry a unique identifier, but we did not read what it must be.
- **Visitors.** Rule 47.51(a) applies to whoever has the aircraft for 28 days or longer; how it applies to a shorter visit is not stated.
- **Part 71 prohibited and restricted areas.** The maps were not read.
- **Distances on our map.** The 3 nm circle is drawn from the centre of every airport in OurAirports, not from each aerodrome reference point, so it is a guide. Rule 102.55 also uses 5 km from a controlled aerodrome's reference point, which the map does not show.
