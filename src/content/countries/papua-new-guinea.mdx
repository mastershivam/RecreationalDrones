This guide rests on excerpts of CASA PNG's Part 101 (14 December 2020) and Part 102 (2 November 2021) that a web search returned
on 26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves**, and later amendments may exist. Check CASA PNG for any change.

## Not covered yet

- **Fees, minimum age, penalties, Remote ID, insurance and visitors.**
- **Pilot licensing:** flying above 400 ft or within 3 nautical miles of an aerodrome reference point needs an extra qualification, such as a radio operator certificate or a flight crew licence (a remote pilot licence needs a training course).
- **The 3 nautical mile circle on our map** is drawn from the centre of every airport in OurAirports, so it is a guide.
