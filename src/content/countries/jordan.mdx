This guide follows CARC's English drones page, its Drones Operations Approval Application Form and its Part DDP, read on 26 October 2026
from web archive copies. The live CARC site did not load for us. The drones page is an archive copy from 7 June 2026, and Part DDP
is a copy from 2 May 2025. Check CARC for any change.

## Which rules apply

Part DDP sets requirements for the design and production of drones intended for CARC's open category, and for remote identification
add-ons. It does not apply to drones used only indoors, or to drones used by the military and security forces. It also says which
drones must be certified. It does not set who may fly a drone, so we have not established which category an ordinary flight falls in.

## Approval for operations

The Drones Operations Approval Application Form (CARC Form 25-0010, amendment 1, effective October 2019) sets these conditions
for the operations it covers (our reading of the English text):

- Apply at least 10 working days ahead to the Ministry of Interior, which coordinates with the Royal Jordanian Air Force and CARC.
- Give the location of operations to within a 500 m radius, and the time in local time.
- Fly at most 400 ft (about 122 m) above ground, or 150 ft (about 46 m) within the city of Amman.
- Fly for at most 3 hours.
- Use a drone of at most 25 kg, powered by electricity only.
- Have geo-fencing and electronic identification on the drone.
- Be an operator aged at least 21, who is trained or qualified for the operation.
- Give priority to manned aircraft.
- Supply a copy of your insurance, passport and qualifications.

The form also has sections for approvals from the Jordan Armed Forces, the Media Commission and other agencies. The form does not say which
operations need it, so we cannot say whether a recreational flight does.
