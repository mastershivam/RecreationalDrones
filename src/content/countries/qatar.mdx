This guide follows three sources we read on 26 October 2026: the CAA's unmanned aircraft page, the CAA's FAQ, and Law No. 10 of 2026 on unmanned aircraft (Official Gazette issue 12 of 2026), published as a PDF on the CAA's site. The law is in Arabic. The article numbers and figures below are our translation and our reading of the page images. Check the CAA for any change.

## Who needs a licence

- **Everyone who flies:** no person may operate, pilot, assist in operating or supervise a drone without a licence from the CAA (Article 9). Article 5 bars designing, making, importing or operating drones without a licence too.
- **Licence conditions** (Article 6): the applicant is a company, sole establishment or club, not bankrupt, with managers of good conduct and qualified staff, and third-party liability insurance for the drone.
- **Application and decision** (Article 7): on the CAA's form, with the documents it requires. The CAA decides within 30 days. A silent refusal counts as a refusal.
- **Term** (Article 8): three calendar years, renewable.

## Insurance, age and the CAA's conditions

- The CAA's drone page sets a minimum operating age of 18, a letter of authorisation showing the type of activity, a maximum of five drones per entity, and no modifications or attachments to a drone.
- The CAA's FAQ says hobby flying goes through the Qatar R/C Sport Center: a hobby licence and registration of the aircraft. The law we read does not mention that centre. We could not reconcile the two, so ask the CAA which applies to you.

## Penalties

- **Operating without a licence** (Article 5, and Article 9): up to seven years in prison, a fine of up to QAR 300,000, or both (Article 22).
- **Compounding table** (the schedule to the law): the amount to settle an offence without going to court. For operating a drone without a licence, the table gives QAR 75,000 for a natural person and QAR 100,000 for a company. For a drone that is not registered with the CAA, it gives QAR 15,000 for a natural person and QAR 20,000 for a company.

- **Licence and registration fees.** We did not read the fee article.
- **Height and distance limits, airport and no-fly areas.** We found no published distance or no-fly map. The regulation leaves zones, altitudes and routes to the CAA.
- **Remote ID and visitors.** Not in the articles we read.
