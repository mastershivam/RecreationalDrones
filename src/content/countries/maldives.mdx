This guide follows the Maldives CAA's page on flying remotely piloted aircraft, and a drone permit application form from the Ministry of
Defence (MNDF) that the CAA's site hosts, both read on 26 October 2026. Check the CAA for any change.

## Approval

The CAA page sets out three steps: MNDF approval, depending on the area; then the CAA's own application, with a copy of the MNDF permit;
then any approval from other parties. The CAA says all drone operations are reviewed case by case, since seaplanes fly under visual
flight rules across the islands and fixed boundaries are hard to set.

## Where you can fly

- **Height:** below 400 feet, according to the CAA page.
- **MNDF form:** applications at least three official working days ahead, with a map showing exact coordinates. In urban areas the
  flight radius is 50 m, over water 150 m, and the maximum height is 120 m.
- **Security zones and airports:** the CAA page links to a government guidance page on drones in security zones and airports. We could
  not open it, so its rules are not stated here.
