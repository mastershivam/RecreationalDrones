This guide follows the Tanzania Civil Aviation Authority's drone page and its frequently asked questions, which we opened and read on 26 October 2026.
The TCAA's site also lists Civil Aviation (Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems) Regulations, 2026. We did not read that text, so its rules may differ from what is set out here. Check the TCAA for any change.

## Where and how you can fly

The TCAA's FAQ gives these limits:

- **Permit:** every drone operation needs a TCAA permit, whatever the purpose. Recreational drones are allowed, but only with that permit.
- **Height:** up to 120 m (400 ft) above ground level. More needs a specific TCAA authorisation.
- **Night:** no flying at night without a special TCAA permit, which requires lighting and extra safety steps.
- **Near airports:** the FAQ says flying near airports has lower altitude limits. It gives no distance.

## Where you cannot fly without permission

As the FAQ lists them: near military installations, the State House, prisons, airports and airfields, government offices, national parks and crowded public places. Each of these needs special permission from the TCAA, and national parks also need the Park Authority's permit.

## Penalties

The FAQ says illegal operation can bring substantial fines, confiscation of the drone, criminal prosecution, and imprisonment in serious cases. Penalties are harsher where safety was endangered. It gives no fine amounts.
