This guide follows the Namibia Civil Aviation Authority's RPAS forms page, which we opened and read ourselves on 26 October 2026.
The page states the registration, approval and national park rules, but gives no fees, and it links no Part 101 text. The
NCAA's RPAS page says "coming soon", and the Government Gazette of 16 April 2026 that some listings cite returns a 404 error
on the NCAA site, so we could not read it. Check the NCAA for any change.

## In Namibia

- **Authority:** [Namibia Civil Aviation Authority (NCAA), RPAS forms page](https://ncaa.com.na/index.php/document-downloads/other-categories/forms-applications/flight-operations/rpas-forms).
- **Rules:** Part 101, which the forms page cites but we could not open.

## Registration and approval

According to the NCAA's forms page, all drones, recreational or commercial, must be registered under Part 101, and all flights
need prior written approval. Foreigners must apply at least 60 days before arriving. Flying in national parks is strictly
prohibited.
