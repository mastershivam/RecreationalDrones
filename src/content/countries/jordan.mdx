not open them ourselves.** Check CARC for any change.

## Which rules apply

Part DDP sets open, specific and certified categories. An operation that does not meet all the open-category conditions is in the specific category and needs CARC's operational authorisation; some, such as flights over assemblies of people, are certified. It does not cover drones used only indoors or for military and security purposes.
