This guide follows the BHDCA's drone page, in English, which excerpts the 2020 Rulebook (Official Gazette 51/20). We read the
page on 26 October 2026. Its footer is dated 2021. We did not open the Rulebook itself, and we do not know whether it has been
amended. Check the BHDCA for any change.

The page keeps two sets of rules apart, and the figures differ between them. The 500 m setback and the 50 m distance from
gatherings are for non-commercial flights. The 100 m setback and the 120 m ceiling are for commercial flights.

## Where you cannot fly

- **Non-commercial flights:** no flying over people; at least 30 m from people, animals, structures, vehicles, roads, railways,
  waterways and power lines; at least 50 m from a public gathering; and at least 500 m from industrial zones, embassies,
  government facilities and institutions, and other public buildings.
- **Commercial flights:** the same 30 m clearance from people and structures, but 100 m from industrial zones, embassies,
  government buildings and other public buildings. They may fly up to 120 m above the ground. At night they must not fly over a
  group of people or important buildings.
- **Border strip:** no flying within 300 m of the border, in either category, without the Border Police's prior consent.
- **Airports:** the page gives the 1.5 km rule under commercial flights only. A flight within 1.5 km of an airport or control
  zone needs the prior consent of air traffic control or the airspace management cell. The page gives no airport distance for
  non-commercial flights.

## Not covered yet

- **Fees, minimum age, penalties and Remote ID.** The page does not give them.
- **Visitors.** The page covers foreign army, police and customs drones, which need approval from the competent Bosnian
  institution. It does not say what a private visitor must do.
- **Drones under 249 g.** The Rulebook starts at 249 g, and the page does not say what applies below it.
- **Airport distance for non-commercial flights.** The map's 1.5 km circle for every airport comes from the commercial rule, so
  it may overstate what applies to a recreational flight. This needs a decision for the lead, in `src/lib/airport-rules.ts`.
