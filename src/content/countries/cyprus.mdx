Cyprus applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then what the Department of Civil Aviation (DCA) of Cyprus adds. The national part follows the
Department's drone website (drones.gov.cy) pages on registration, the open category and geographical zones, read on
25 October 2026. Check it for any change.

<EasaRules />

## In Cyprus

- **Register:** [the Department's UAS portal](https://uas.drones.gov.cy/index.php/sessions/login).
- **Where you can fly:** [the Department's geographical zones map](https://drones.gov.cy/geo-zones-map/), a downloadable file.
- **Authority:** [Department of Civil Aviation of Cyprus](https://drones.gov.cy/).

## Registration

The Department says you register as an operator if a drone weighs more than 250 g, or is lighter but has a camera or
other equipment that can capture personal data. You register the operator, not the drone.

- **Fee and term:** EUR 15, valid for 3 years, then you register again.
- **Age:** "You must register ONLY if you are 18 years of age and above." Otherwise a parent, guardian or other
  responsible person registers as the operator for you.
- **Pilot exam:** the A1/A3 online training and exam cost EUR 15 in total. There are 40 multiple-choice questions, the pass
  mark is 75% and you get 3 tries. The certificate lasts 5 years. The minimum age for remote pilots is 16, and under 16
  you may fly if supervised by someone 16 or older. A C0 drone under 250 g needs no exam.
- **Your number:** it must be displayed on every drone so that it cannot be erased, and uploaded to the drone if it can
  take it.
- **Visitors from outside the EU:** if this is your first time flying in an EU country, you must register as an operator
  with the Department, and hold a remote pilot certificate issued by an EU country. A visitor can pay only by Visa card.

## Where you cannot fly without permission

Cyprus sets its geographical zones by ministerial decree 190/2024, as amended by 123/2025, under Article 15 of Regulation
2019/947. There are two kinds, restricted (yellow on the map) and prohibited. The Department says flights in a restricted
zone can only be made after authorisation from the zone's administrator, which you request as the operator.

- **Larnaca and Paphos airports:** each has two zones. In Zone A (red) "NO UAS operations are allowed", and the page
  warns of heavy fines and imprisonment. In Zone B (yellow), open-category flying is restricted: you need the Department's
  permission by sending its airport airspace reservation request form 7 working days ahead (the page also says 10 for
  Larnaca and Paphos), and third-party liability insurance of EUR 1,000,000 is mandatory. The page gives no distances; check
  the zone map.
- **Visitors from outside the EU** are told to keep at least 8 km from airports and heliports. The Department's airport
  zones page gives no 8 km figure, so we do not know how this relates to Zones A and B.
- **Preinstalled maps:** the Department warns that most DJI drones come with a map for Cyprus that "IS NOT THE RIGHT
  ONE".
- **Other places:** the Department's rules of the air say not to overfly crowds, to keep your drone in sight, and not to
  take photos, video or sound of people without their permission. It also asks you not to fly where emergency services
  are working.

## Penalties

The Department says only that "Violation of the law constitutes an offence and offenders will be liable to penalties".
It gives no amounts for the open category. For Zone A at the airports it warns of "heavy fines and imprisonment".

## Not covered yet

- **Insurance.** The pages we read give no general rule for a light drone, only the Zone B requirement above.
- **Penalty amounts** and the Cypriot law behind them. The Department's regulations page lists only EU rules.
- **Airport distances.** The airport zones page gives no distance; the Department's zones file is the source.
- **Where else flying is banned.** The zones are in the Department's map and files, and we have not copied them. Its
