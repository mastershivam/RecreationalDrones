This guide rests on excerpts of SHGM's registration system and its drone instruction (SHT-İHA, the 2026 text) that a web search
returned on 26 October 2026. The pages are in Turkish and the translations are ours. **We could not open the pages or the PDF
themselves**, so read them before you rely on this. Check SHGM for any change.

## Registration

SHGM's system says pilots who want to fly drones of 500 g and above in Turkish airspace must register and get SHGM approval.
Owners enter their ID number, name, address and contact details. A drone bought in Türkiye must be registered the same day,
and one brought from abroad or transferred within three days; each gets a square code to fix to the drone. The FAQ says an
operator must be at least 18. The 2019 instruction did not require training for hobby or sport drones.

## Where and how you can fly

An older draft annex for hobby model aircraft says to fly below 400 ft, away from obstacles and always in sight. We could not
confirm it in the final text.
