Lithuania applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then links to Lithuania's own authority for what is national. The Transport Competence
Agency's registration portal (sertifikatai.tka.lt) was read on 26 October 2026. Its pages give the registration
steps but no fees, terms or rules, so this guide adds only those steps.

<EasaRules />

## In Lithuania

- **Register:** [Lithuania's registration portal](https://sertifikatai.tka.lt/en/login-foreign).
- **Where you can fly:** [Lithuania's map of geographical zones](https://ltsa.lrv.lt/lt/veiklos-sritys/oro-transportas-1/bepilociai-orlaiviai/neskraidymo-zonu-zemelapis). The map is in Lithuanian.
- **Authority:** [Transport Competence Agency of Lithuania](https://www.tka.lt/).
- **Note:** The no-fly map EASA links to is in Lithuanian.

## Not covered yet

- **Lithuania's national rules.** The agency's main site (tka.lt) and the aviation authority's map page refused our requests, so we have not read Lithuania's own drone rules. What we say here is only what EASA says applies everywhere, plus what the registration portal asks for.
- **Fees and how long registration lasts.** Neither the portal nor the EASA pages give them, so both are unverified.
- **Minimum age.** EASA's 16 may be lowered to 12 by a state. The portal's registration form asks for a parental consent for a minor, but gives no age.
- **Insurance, penalties and local bans.** Unverified. Check the authority above before you fly.
- **Where flying is banned.** Lithuania's geographical zones are on the map linked above, and we have not copied them.
