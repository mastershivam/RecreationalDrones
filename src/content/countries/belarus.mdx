The Department of Aviation's own page (caa.gov.by, Russian) was not reachable live: its certificate failed our check. We read an
Internet Archive copy of it, saved on 16 June 2026, on 26 October 2026. Our translation from the Russian is below. We did not open
Presidential Decree No. 297 or the Council of Ministers resolution on state accounting of drones (No. 972, as amended). We could not
read a clean copy of either. Check the Department before you travel with a drone.

## Registration

- **Electronic only:** from 1 January 2026, the Department registers civil drones only in its electronic system, at uas.gov.by.
- **Who can log in:** the system is entered with an electronic digital signature, and the page says it is open only to legal entities
  and sole proprietors.
- **Permit first:** the registration documents include the Department's radio-equipment operating permit, issued under Appendix 32 to
  the aviation rules (Resolution No. 111 of 29 December 2022).

## Not covered yet

- **Whether private individuals may register or fly**, which is the question Decree No. 297 answers. This decides whether the level
  is 4.
- **Penalties.** A bill on criminal and administrative penalties was reported in 2025. We did not confirm whether it was adopted.
- **Weight classes, fees, height and distance limits, Remote ID, insurance and visitors.**
