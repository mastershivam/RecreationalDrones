This guide rests on CAASI's drone page and its registration form CAASI 24101/01 (Issue 1, 2016), which we opened and read on
26 October 2026. **We did not find Solomon Islands' own Part 101 or Part 102.** The copies CAASI hosts are New Zealand CAA
consolidations, as their title pages say, and the advisory circular AC 101-1 that CAASI hosts is a New Zealand CAA document
(Revision 1, 2015). So we use none of them for Solomon Islands law. Check CAASI for any rule.
