This guide follows the SLCAA's Part 6B (Unmanned Aircraft Systems, 5 February 2024) and Part 28 (Aerial Work, 5 February 2024),
both read on 26 October 2026 from the SLCAA's own PDFs. The text is in English. Check the SLCAA for any change.

## Registration

Every unmanned aircraft operated in Sierra Leone must be registered (2.2.1). The SLCAA's homepage showed no drone page, so we
did not find the registration process, its fee or its form.

## Where and how you can fly

For flying within the standard conditions (2.3), you need no authorisation:

- **Line of sight:** the drone must stay within visual line of sight of the operator.
- **Height:** at or below 120 m (400 ft) above ground, by day.
- **People:** not within 30 m of anyone not involved in the operation, and not over a populated area.
- **Areas:** not in a prohibited or restricted area, and not within 4 km of a controlled aerodrome's movement area.
- **Mass:** no operation of a drone over 25 kg (2.18.1); from 15 kg to 25 kg it must be built or approved by an approved person or organisation (2.18.2).

Near aerodromes, 2.11.1 bars operating on or within 4 km of an uncontrolled aerodrome unless a written agreement, an observer
and a height limit are in place, and within 4 km of a controlled aerodrome unless air traffic control authorises it.

## Other operations

Any operation other than under the regulation needs a UAS authorisation or operator certificate before flying (4.5.1). The
application is made under 4.6, and a fee is payable (4.5.3). Aerial work for a Sierra Leone operator uses the form in
IS 1.1(F) of Part 28. A foreign operator uses the form IS 8.1.1(a), which Part 28 names (1.1(g)) but does not reproduce.

## Not covered yet

- **Fees:** the registration fee and the authorisation fee are not stated in the text we read.
- **Minimum age** for recreational flying: not stated. A remote pilot licence needs age 18 (3.2.1), but the licence is not needed outside 4 km of an aerodrome (2.20.1).
- **Insurance** for recreational flying: not required in the Part 6B text we read. Part 28 requires an insurance certificate for aerial work.
- **Penalties:** not read.
- **Remote ID and online systems:** we found no online registration system and no remote identification rule.
- **Airport distances:** the 4 km figure in 2.11.1 and 2.3 is the clearest candidate for the airport-rules file, with its source.
