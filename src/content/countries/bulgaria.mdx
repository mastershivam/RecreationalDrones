Bulgaria applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then links to Bulgaria's own authority for what is national.

<EasaRules />

## In Bulgaria

- **Register:** with Civil Aviation Administration of Bulgaria ([its website](https://www.caa.bg/)).
- **Where you can fly:** [Bulgaria's map of geographical zones](https://www.caa.bg/bg/category/633/7062).
- **Authority:** [Civil Aviation Administration of Bulgaria](https://www.caa.bg/).
- **Note:** EASA's list links to `drones.caa.bg` for registration, but that address did not load when we checked. Start from the Civil Aviation Administration's site.

## Not covered yet

- **Bulgaria's national rules.** We have not read Bulgaria's own drone rules, so what we say here is only what EASA says
  applies everywhere. Fees, how long registration lasts, the minimum age (a state may lower EASA's 16 to 12),
  insurance, penalties and any local bans are all unverified. Check the authority above before you fly.
- **Where flying is banned.** Bulgaria's geographical zones are on the map linked above, and we have not copied them.
