This guide follows the Civil Aviation Directorate's FAQ on foreign drone operations and its unmanned aircraft page, read on 26 October 2026. Its
English rulebook PDF is named after the 2015 gazette, but its text is the rulebook signed on 23 December 2019 and applicable from 15 February 2020,
which repeals the 2015 rulebook. The CAD says a rule from 2026 applies, and we could not read it. Check the CAD for any change.

## Where and how you can fly

The rulebook we read (its Articles 7 to 16) sets these limits:

- **Altitude:** 100 m above ground, unless the CAD approves more and the airspace is allocated.
- **Range:** at most 500 m horizontally from the operator, and within the operator's line of sight.
- **People:** at least 30 m from people. No flying over people, except that the CAD may allow it for a category 1 drone that cannot gain more than 80 J.
- **Aerodromes:** no flying within 5 km of the listed aerodromes, or 1.5 km of others, without CAD approval. Inside an aerodrome's boundary it needs the operator's approval.
- **Restricted areas and buildings:** no flying within 500 m of government, military, diplomatic, energy and transport sites, without the owner's permission.
- **Night:** only with prior CAD approval.

Drones of more than 150 kg are outside the rulebook. The rulebook also sets flying regions for each category, which we did not summarise.

## Registration

The CAD's FAQ says a foreign drone needs no registration in Serbia: "UAS registration is not required". A foreign drone still needs a flight
authorisation for every flight, whatever its weight.

Serbian operators follow the rulebook's register rules (Article 4): drones of 4 kg or more must be entered, and so must lighter drones when used for
a listed purpose. The CAD's web platform for drones is marked "soon" on its unmanned aircraft page, with no link.

## Age and training

The operator must be an adult who is medically fit and has passed the proficiency tests (Article 20). The rulebook lets a minor over 15 fly a
category 1 drone in a simple operation, or a younger one under direct adult supervision. The rulebook does not state an age number for other
operations.
