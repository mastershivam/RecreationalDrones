This guide follows the Aviation Administration of Kazakhstan's registration page, read on 26 October 2026, and its January 2021
post announcing the rules for operating drones, read the same day. We read the page's own text. We did not open the operating
rules, the Code of Administrative Offences or the restricted-area map the page links to, so the flight rules below come from
the post and the page only. Check the Administration for any change.

## Registration

- **Who:** any unmanned aircraft of 0.25 kg or more, and any system with data-collection equipment whatever its weight.
  Systems of 750 kg or more are registered by the state instead.
- **Where:** the eLicense.kz portal, after a technical compliance declaration to the Administration through eOtinish.kz.
- **Processing:** 10 working days, plus 5 if extra assessment is needed.
- **Fee:** free.
- **Term:** 5 years, or the length of the lease agreement.
- **Visitors:** a foreign citizen needs an EDS, which is issued in person at a Kazakh embassy or consulate. The page says the
  service is free and usually takes up to two business days. The page also asks for a notarised translation of the name
  into Kazakh or Russian if the documents are not in those languages.

## Tail number

The number starts with "Q", then a take-off weight classifier, a design type classifier and a registration number. It goes on
in 3 places for up to 1.5 kg and 5 places above that, in characters at least 5 mm high that stay legible when wet, before the
drone is used.

## Flying

- **Densely populated areas:** a flight over a city or town's densely populated areas needs the Administration's permission,
  according to the 2021 post. The post does not give the procedure, and the operating rules that set it were not read.
- **Protected objects:** the page says that flying over protected objects without approval from the Ministry of Defence or
  the State Security Service can lead to confiscation.

## Penalties

The page cites a fine of 40 MCI under Article 569 of the Code of Administrative Offences for failing to register. It says
confiscation is possible for some violations and that fines can rise for repeat violations. We did not read the Code.
