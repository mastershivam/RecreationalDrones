Austria applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This guide gives
those rules, then Austria's own rules from Austro Control's Dronespace pages, read on 26 October 2026. Our summaries of
the German pages are our own translation. The Dronespace pages show no date, so check them for changes.

<EasaRules />

## In Austria

- **Register:** [Austro Control's Dronespace registration portal](https://utm.dronespace.at/pm/), or its
  [information page](https://www.dronespace.at/registrierung/allgemeine_informationen).
- **Where you can fly:** Dronespace's zone pages, for [control zones](https://www.dronespace.at/geo_zonen/kontrollzonen),
  [aerodrome zones](https://www.dronespace.at/geo_zonen/flugplatzzonen) and
  [restricted areas](https://www.dronespace.at/geo_zonen/flugbeschraenkungsgebiete). The zones are shown in the Dronespace system.
- **Authority:** [Austro Control](https://www.austrocontrol.at/).

### Registration

- **Fee and term:** EUR 46.80, valid for 3 years. Renewal costs the same, and you keep your number.
- **Age:** 18 to register as an operator, and you must be fully legally capable. Austro Control's FAQ says the minimum age
  for the competence certificate is 16, and that from 16 a person may act as a pilot, but an adult must complete the
  registration. The map's age field is the 18 for registering.
- **Where you register:** Austria's requirement is a main residence or business seat in Austria. The EU rule is that you
  register where you live. A non-EU operator may register in Austria for a first UAS flight there. The FAQ says short
  stays by non-EU operators are covered too.
- **Number:** a 16-digit number, shown on every drone and entered with the 3-digit PIN into the drone's Remote ID system if
  it has one. Austro Control says only enforcement authorities can link the number to a person.

### Insurance

Austro Control says the insurance duty under the Luftfahrtgesetz stays, and that cover must be at least 750,000 special
drawing rights. This includes the DJI Mini series if it must be registered. You enter the policy number when you register
and do not upload the policy. Its page warns that insurance for flights in other EU countries may need to meet other
requirements.

### Where you cannot fly without permission

Austro Control's zone pages say these need permission:

- **Control zones** (around Graz, Innsbruck, Klagenfurt, Linz, Salzburg and Wien-Schwechat): only with the consent of
  the air traffic control unit, requested by filing a flight plan in Dronespace. A drone under 250 g that stays at or
  below 30 m above ground is exempt.
- **Aerodrome zones:** an operating permit (Betriebsbewilligung) from Austro Control, under § 18 (4) of the
  Luftverkehrsregeln 2014. The pages give no distance.
- **Restricted areas:** Vienna (LO R 15) and the Rheindelta (LO R 18) need a paid Austro Control permit, with the same
  exemption for a drone under 250 g at or below 30 m. That exemption is lost if an aerodrome without a zone lies in the
  flight area. Neusiedler See (LO R 16) needs no Austro Control permit in August and September, but nature-protection
  approval from the Burgenland government is needed before any flight.

### Penalties

A breach can be an administrative offence under section 169 of the Luftfahrtgesetz. The district administrative authority
can impose fines of up to EUR 22,000, depending on the breach.

## Not covered yet

- **Distances around airports.** Austro Control's pages give none for control or aerodrome zones; the map is the source.
  The circles on our map are not an Austrian rule.
- **Model aircraft, privacy and data protection, and night flying.** The pages we read do not cover them for the open
  category.
- **Military zones and temporary event restrictions.** We have not read them.
- **Nature-protection rules** outside the Neusiedler See area, and any nature reserves.
- **Fines for uninsured flying or for a missing number.** The FAQ gives only the general maximum of EUR 22,000.
- **The A2 and specific-category rules**, and the detail of Remote ID for older drones.
- **Where flying is banned.** Austria's zones are on the Dronespace map linked above, and we have not copied them.
