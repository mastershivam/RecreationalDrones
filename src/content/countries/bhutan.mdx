This guide is based on two BCAA documents we read ourselves: the BCAA's drone FAQ (read through a page summary, so the quotes here are paraphrased) and the UAS Regulation 2017 as hosted on bcaa.gov.bt. The regulation is Chapter 12, Issue 01. Its pages show two revision dates, 22 September 2017 and 1 May 2017, so the BCAA should confirm which is current. We did not open Appendix II as a separate document. The clauses cited here are from the regulation itself.

## Which rules apply

- **Who may fly:** the regulation's scope clause (4.12.1.1 f) says UAS are permitted only for government organisations. The FAQ says private individuals and companies may not own or use drones, and that imports need BCAA approval, which is given only to government agencies. A tourist's drone is seized, and the local tour operator can be fined for not warning the tourist.
- **Penalties:** operating without BCAA approval carries a fine of Nu 20,000 to Nu 50,000 and seizure of the drone (clause 4.12.21.1). Breaching a person's privacy or property is also an offence (clause 4.12.21.2).
- **Limits:** maximum height 300 ft (90 m) above ground, 50 m lateral distance from people, buildings and animals, and 200 m from heritage and archaeological sites (clauses 4.12.10.1 to 4.12.10.3). The regulation applies to drones up to 6 kg, and above 6 kg only in special circumstances.

## Where the sources disagree

- The regulation's registration clause (4.12.5.2) makes a drone eligible for registration if a person or a government entity owns it. The scope clause (4.12.1.1 f) and the FAQ allow only government organisations. We have followed the stricter reading.
- A search excerpt (not an authority page) said another BCAA FAQ allows any type of drone to fly under set conditions. The FAQ page we read says the opposite, so we have not used the excerpt.

## Not covered yet

- **Remote ID, validity of the registration itself, and visitor rules beyond the import rule.** Tourists' drones are seized (clause 4.12.21.1), and any import needs prior BCAA approval (clause 4.12.4.9).
- **The BCAA's "negative list" of no-fly areas**, which we did not find in the pages we read.
- **Distances from airports:** clause 4.12.10.3 (c) gives 5 km from a controlled aerodrome, unless the air traffic control section authorises the flight. The map does not draw that circle yet.
