This guide follows IDAC's "Vuela Seguro" drone guide: its main page, its registration-requirements page and its FAQ.
All are in Spanish and were read on 26 October 2026 through a page-fetch tool that returned quoted text (our
translations). The pages show a footer year of 2026 but no effective date. The registration rules (RAD 48) date from
June 2024 and the operations rules (RAD 107) from April 2025, according to the links on the guide. Check IDAC for any change.

## Registration

Drones of 0.25 kg (0.55 lb) or more must be registered and labelled, under RAD 48. The main page says registration is
needed "desde 0.55 lb (0.25 kg) o más". The FAQ says drones between 0.25 kg and 25 kg must be labelled. Drones under
249 g need no licence or insurance, according to the main page.

## Remote pilot licence

The main page and the FAQ both say that operators of drones over 0.25 kg need an IDAC remote pilot licence (RAD 107.23).
The requirements are 18 years or over, an IDAC-approved initial course, Spanish, and a theory and a practical test.
Renewal needs an IDAC-approved recurrent course (RAD 107.103). The licence is not limited to commercial operators; the
recreational route asks for a copy of it. This is why we rate the country level 4, not level 3.

## Where you may fly

Outside Class G airspace, a drone needs IDAC's prior authorisation and coordination with air traffic control (RAD 107.57).
Flying over government or military areas needs permission from the same department (RAD 107.61). The FAQ says you must
not fly over people unless they are directly involved or sheltered by a covered structure or a parked vehicle (RAD
107.55), and not at night without anti-collision lighting visible at 5 km (RAD 107.43). The height ceiling is 400 ft
(122 m) above ground, and ground speed should not exceed 87 knots (RAD 107.67).

A foreign national may fly only if a Dominican company hires them and requests it.

## Insurance

Third-party surface liability cover is required from 0.55 lb (0.25 kg) (RAD 107.69). For a recreational drone between
0.55 and 4.4 lb, IDAC accepts a simple sworn joint-liability declaration. Above 4.4 lb, the FAQ gives USD 2,000 as the
minimum.

## Penalties and incidents

IDAC's FAQ says sanctions may include fines, suspension or revocation of the remote pilot licence, and other legal action
(RAD 107.5 and 107.83). It gives no amounts. Accidents with collision danger, serious injury or property damage over
RD$25,000 must be reported within 10 calendar days (RAD 107.9).
