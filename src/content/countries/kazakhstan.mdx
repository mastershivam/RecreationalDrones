This guide rests on excerpts of the Aviation Administration's registration page and a post on the operating rules that a web
search returned on 26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** The thresholds in its sources disagree, so read the
current text. Check the Administration for any change.

## Penalties

The page cites a fine of 40 MCI under Article 569 of the Code of Administrative Offences for failing to register, and says confiscation is possible for violations such as flying over protected sites without approval.
