This guide follows the Civil Aviation of Madagascar's (ACM) drone page and its registration form, FORM-ACM/DRG/UAS-003, dated
7 January 2025. We opened and read them ourselves. The form and the page are in French and the translations are ours. We could not
read the text of the ACM's notice on mandatory registration, which the drone page lists, or the RAM 10 000 regulation the form
cites, so we did not read the flying rules. Check the ACM for any change.

## In Madagascar

- **Authority:** [Civil Aviation of Madagascar (ACM), drone page](https://www.acm.mg/drone/).
- **Registration:** [the ACM's UAS registration form](https://www.acm.mg/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/formulaire_enregistrement_UAS_ed_01_amdt_01-1.pdf), sent by email or handed in.
- **Notices:** [ACM public notices](https://www.acm.mg/avis-au-public/), which list the registration notice by title.

## Registration

The form is for every UAS operator or owner. It asks for the applicant's ID or passport, a residence certificate, proof of
ownership, the aircraft's make, model, serial number, maximum take-off mass, maximum height and flight time, and the planned use
(leisure or professional). The applicant signs a declaration that the information is accurate. The form's note says the operator
pays the registration fee only once the ACM sets up a site for definitive registration. The form gives no fee amount.
