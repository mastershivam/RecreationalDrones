This guide rests on excerpts of the Monaco government's drone pages and the Journal de Monaco that a web search returned on 26
October 2026. The texts are in French and the translations are ours. **We did not open them ourselves.** The regulatory framework
came into force on 6 February 2022; we did not check for later amendments. Check the DAC for any change.

## Where you cannot fly

As summarised: not above the Place du Palais and the Prince's Palace, nor within 150 m of the heliport, unless the Director authorises it. Flights must be in direct sight, and drones with a thermal engine are banned. Very light model aircraft under 100 g are freely allowed, except near the Palace and the heliport. Authorised State personnel may neutralise a drone flying without the required authorisation.
