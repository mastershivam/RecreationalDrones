This guide follows SACAA's drones page, re-read on 26 October 2026, the Thirty-third Amendment of the Civil Aviation Regulations
(Government Gazette 55226, 21 August 2026, which came into operation on publication), and the Thirty-Fifth Amendment's fee
schedule (Government Gazette 55257, 28 August 2026, from 1 October 2026). The page's footer reads "Copyright 2025" and it gives no
date for its text.

## Which rules apply

SACAA says private use means an individual's personal and private purposes with no commercial outcome, interest or gain, with
a Class 1A or 1B drone (under 7 kg and under 15 kJ of impact energy). Its table of approvals marks every approval (operator
certificate, letter of authority, pilot licence and certificate of registration) "N/A" for private operation. The gazette text
matches: private operations are exempt from Subparts 2, 4 and 6 of Part 101, may fly only in R-VLOS with a Class 1A or 1B
aircraft, and may not carry dangerous goods. The page also says Part 101 excludes model aircraft, which "operated at approved
SAMAA airfields only", and that "operations as a hobbyist are subject to the terms of Part 94".

## Registration

The registration rule is quoted from the page: no UAS may be operated "unless such UAS has been issued with a certificate of
registration by the Director". Its quoted text does not mention the August 2026 amendment, so the gazette governs where they differ.
The amendment exempts private operation from Subparts 2, 4 and 6. The gazette does not print the list of Part 101 sections, so
that 101.02.4 sits in Subpart 2 is our inference from the numbering (other Parts' Subpart 2 sections follow the same pattern), not a quoted line.

For non-private flying, the application is form CA-47R1, in the original. The fee is R922.00 for registering a UA, under "Fees
relating to Part 101 (Unmanned Aircraft System)" in the Thirty-Fifth Amendment (from 1 October 2026). The page itself does not
give the amount.

## Where and how you can fly

For private flying, SACAA says:

- **Height:** do not fly higher than **150 ft** (about 45 m) from the ground, unless SACAA approves.
- **Aerodromes:** do not fly **10 km or closer** to an aerodrome (airport, helipad or airfield), unless SACAA approves.
- **People and property:** not **50 m or closer** to any person or group (sports fields, stadiums, schools, social events), a
  public road, or any property without the owner's permission.
- **Line of sight:** within sight at all times, and within 500 m of you and below the highest obstacle within 300 m. The gazette
  requires R-VLOS for private flying, but does not say whether night flying is allowed. The page says "R-VLOS (day and night)" in
  one place and "daylight and clear weather" in its list of rules, and we could not settle it.
- **Other limits:** not near manned aircraft, and not in controlled, restricted or prohibited airspace, or beside or over a
  nuclear power plant, prison, police station, court, national key point or strategic installation. Over 7 kg needs SACAA's
  approval.

## Not covered yet

- **Penalties, insurance, minimum age and Remote ID:** none is on the pages we read.
- **Part 94, the hobbyist regime:** the page says hobbyist flying is subject to it, and the gazette only amends it. Whether it adds
  duties for a flyer for fun is unverified, which is why the level is 1 but not final.
- **Visitors:** the page says nothing about flying a drone you bring into the country.
- **The 10 km circle on our map** is drawn from every airport's centre in OurAirports, so it shows more places than the
  rule covers. SACAA's own maps and the AIP show the real airspace.
