This guide follows the DGAC's drone page and its frequently asked questions, both in Spanish and read on 26 October 2026
through a page-fetch tool that returned quoted text (our translations). The drone page's footer says 2023 and the FAQ's
footer says 2024. Neither page gives a last-updated date. Check the DGAC for any change.

## Registration

A recreational drone does not need to be registered. The FAQ answers a question about a drone flown for personal
entertainment: if you follow the basic rules, you need neither registration nor an operator licence. The drone page says
drone registration is required only for assigning registration marks in the operating-certificate processes.

## Where you cannot fly

The drone page says it is prohibited to fly over people, cities or towns, and to operate near airports. The FAQ adds
private property, prisons, and areas where commercial aircraft fly. Neither page gives a distance, so the map draws no
circle for Costa Rica.

## Optional operator licence

The FAQ describes the operator licence as optional. The route is a theory and practical course at an authorised school,
then the DGAC's theory and practical exams at USD 61 each, then an application for the licence at USD 94.
