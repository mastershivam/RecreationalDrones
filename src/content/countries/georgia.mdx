We opened the GCAA's drone platform home page (uas.gov.ge/EN) and its news item of 1 April 2021 on the online registration platform
ourselves on 26 October 2026. We read the Air Code of Georgia through an Internet Archive copy of matsne.gov.ge, because the live
site refused automated access. Article 21 of the Air Code says the rules for registering and flying unmanned aircraft are set by a
normative act of the GCAA. We could not open that act, so the 250 g threshold and the test rules come from the GCAA's own news item,
not from the regulation. We did not use airspace.gov.ge, which the GCAA links to, because its licence rule does not appear on an
authority page we read. Check the GCAA for any change.

## Where and how you can fly

The GCAA's home page gives the open-category conditions. The aircraft must weigh less than 25 kg. Keep it at a safe distance from
people and never fly it over assemblies of people. Keep it in line of sight at all times, except in follow-me mode or with an
observer. Keep it within 120 m of the ground.
