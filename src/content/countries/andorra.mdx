This guide follows the AAA's 2025 drone FAQ, a PDF in Catalan, updated on 11 September 2025 according to the document. We read
its text layer on 26 October 2026. The translations are ours. The FAQ says the operating regulation prevails in any
discrepancy. It cites no law or regulation by number, so we have dropped the earlier citations. We did not open the legal text.
Check the AAA for any change.

## Who must identify and insure

- **Identification:** a drone over 250 g must be identified to the Government, and its data kept up to date. A drone made from
  1 January 2024 must carry active identification.
- **Insurance:** civil liability insurance is compulsory for a drone over 250 g.
- **Pilot competence:** above class C0, or to fly above 50 m, the pilot must show competence for the class of drone and the
  category of flight. In class C0, up to 50 m, no pilot certificate is required.
- **Flight records:** there is no general obligation to send flight records. Commercial open-category operators must send
  operation statistics each year to the ministry for transport.

## Where and how you can fly

- **Open category:** in sight (VLOS), up to 120 m above the ground, not over crowds, and within the zone limits.
- **Non-open flights:** a recreational flight in any category other than open needs authorisation from the ministry for
  transport. The specific category also needs a risk assessment.
- **Restricted zones:** no flying over or inside the perimeter of the listed buildings without express authorisation. The list
  includes government and administrative buildings, the frontier buildings, the courthouse, the prison, the fire station and
  hospital, the national heliport, and the embassies.
- **Natural parks:** Sorteny, Comapedrosa and Madriu-Perafita-Claror are prohibited, unless the environment ministry gives
  express authorisation.
- **Helicopters:** in the open category the FAQ recommends telling Heliand before take-off, with a phone number for the
  flight. It warns that helicopters fly low near the mountains, often under 120 m.
- **Private property:** flying over it is not prohibited, but you must keep your distance and respect privacy. Take off and
  land only on private land with the owner's permission.
