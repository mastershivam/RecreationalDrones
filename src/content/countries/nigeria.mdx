This guide rests on excerpts of the NCAA's Nig.CARs 2023 Part 21 and its advisory circulars that a web search returned on 26
October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** The 2023 text exists in two versions with different effective dates (17 May and
22 September 2023). Check the NCAA for any change.
