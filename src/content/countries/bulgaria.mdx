Bulgaria applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then Bulgaria's own, from the Civil Aviation Administration's (CAA, GD GVA) drone pages,
Ordinance No. N-6 of 11 April 2023 (as amended to 24 December 2025) and the Civil Aviation Act, read on 25 October
2026. Check the CAA for any change.

<EasaRules />

## In Bulgaria

- **Register:** [the CAA's UAS registration portal](https://uas.caa.bg/).
- **Where you can fly:** [the CAA's page of geographical zones](https://www.caa.bg/bg/category/633/7062). It gives a
  downloadable JSON file, not a web map, and see the note below.
- **Authority:** [Civil Aviation Administration of Bulgaria](https://www.caa.bg/).

### Registration

- **Who registers:** Ordinance N-6 (Art. 5) lets a person who normally lives in Bulgaria, a company based there, or a
  person from outside the EU for whom Bulgaria is the first EU state they apply in, register. You cannot be registered
  in more than one EU state.
- **How:** online, on the CAA's portal. The CAA says its drone portal needs a **qualified electronic signature (КЕП)**,
  and that a mobile one can be got for free and fully remotely. It does not say whether a visitor from outside Bulgaria
  can get one.
- **Fee:** a state fee ("държавна такса") is charged, but the CAA pages and the ordinance we read give no amount.
- **Age:** a remote pilot needs to be 16. If the pilot is under 18, a parent or guardian is registered as the operator
  and carries the responsibility. We left the map's age field unverified.
- **Changes:** report any change in your details to the CAA within 10 days.
- **Pilot certificate:** valid for 5 years, and renewed by passing the exam again. A pilot from outside the EU is not
  exempt: the CAA says qualifications from your own country are not recognised in the EU.

### Insurance

The Civil Aviation Act (Art. 19a) requires third-party liability insurance for flying a drone, except for a drone under
20 kg flown only for sport or fun. The ordinance repeats this for hobby flying (Art. 29): insurance is needed for a drone
over 20 kg. The register holds your policy number "if such insurance is required".

### Where you cannot fly

- **Geographical zones:** the CAA says flights go up to 120 m outside the zones, under the open-category rules, and
  inside a zone only on that zone's published conditions. Before each flight you must download the CAA's JSON file of
  zones and load it into your drone's geo-awareness function, and during the flight check every 15 minutes whether a zone
  that affects you has been activated, and end the flight if so. The CAA warns that zones in drone makers' apps do not
  always cover every restricted area in Bulgaria.
- **By law (Civil Aviation Act, Art. 62b, and Ordinance Art. 57):** no flying a drone without permission in a
  restricted or prohibited geographical zone, in airspace structures that are active, in a restricted or danger area in
  the national aeronautical publication, or within the secured perimeter of a public airport. A drone that breaks this can
  be forced down, and the operator pays for the damage.
- **Permission** to fly in such a zone is applied for with a form on the CAA's site. Near an airport, the conditions are
  agreed with air traffic services and the airport operator. The CAA says information on zones activated for low-flying
  aircraft is on the BULATSA B-FLIP site, after you register there.

### Penalties

Under the Civil Aviation Act (Art. 143(1)), flying a drone without a required permission, or in breach of the rules, can
be fined **3,000 to 10,000 leva**, in the Act's own currency (Bulgaria has since adopted the euro, and we did not find the
euro figure). Flying an unregistered drone is also an offence.

## Not covered yet

- **Registration fee.** The state fee is not published on the pages we read, and the amount of the fee for the pilot
  certificate is also unknown.
- **How long operator registration lasts.** The pages we read give no end date.
- **Airport distances and the zones themselves.** The CAA gives them only as a JSON file, which we have not copied. Our map
  circles around airports are not a Bulgarian rule.
- **The CAA's live zone page** returned "403 Forbidden" when we tried it on 25 October 2026. We read an Internet Archive
  copy dated 18 August 2026, which listed the zone file of 30 July 2026, so the file may have been updated since.
- **Privacy rules and model-flying clubs.**
- **The free electronic signature.** The guide says a mobile qualified signature can be got free. The CAA's registration
  page (caa.bg/bg/category/899/29602), re-read on 26 October 2026, mentions yearly fees charged by third-party signature
  providers. We could not read the full sentence, so the "free" claim needs checking before anyone relies on it.
- **The CAA portal (uas.caa.bg).** The page loads a script shell with no readable text, so we could not re-check the
  fee, validity or age on it on 26 October 2026.
