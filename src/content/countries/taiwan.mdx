This guide rests on excerpts of the CAA's drone pages and the Civil Aviation Act that a web search returned on 26 October 2026.
**We did not open them ourselves.** The drone chapter has been in force since 31 March 2020, the Drone Regulations of 1 December
2024 are listed, and a 2026 consultation on amendments is open. Check the CAA for any change.
