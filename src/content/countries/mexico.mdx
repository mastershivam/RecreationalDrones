This guide follows the AFAC's RPAS page (dated 4 September 2020) and NOM-107-SCT3-2019, published in the Diario Oficial de la Federación
on 14 November 2019. The page and the NOM are in Spanish, and the translations are ours. Check the AFAC for any change.

## In Mexico

- **Register:** [the AFAC's RPAS page](https://www.gob.mx/afac/acciones-y-programas/rpas-drones), which gives the email address and form.
- **Rules:** [NOM-107-SCT3-2019](https://www.gob.mx/cms/uploads/attachment/file/602732/nom-107-sct3-2019-201119.pdf).
- **Authority:** [Federal Civil Aviation Agency (AFAC)](https://www.gob.mx/afac/acciones-y-programas/rpas-drones).

## Registration

- **Who:** the NOM's micro class (up to 2 kg) must register above 250 g (5.1.1 a). The small class (over 2 kg to 25 kg) must register (6.1.1 a).
- **Process:** an email to rpas@afac.gob.mx with Apéndice K, proof of ownership and an ID, under NOM 15.3. An RFC tax ID is requested "with homoclave if available". Companies also send their incorporation deed and power of attorney.
- **Time:** the AFAC page says within 10 business days of a complete request.
- **Fee:** neither the AFAC page nor the NOM we read gives a fee.
- **Term:** the NOM gives no validity period for the folio.
- **Minors:** a minor who flies a micro or small recreational drone must have the registration made by an adult, who acts as the operator (NOM 15.3, note 1).

## Visitors

A drone registered abroad, or operated by a foreign operator, may not fly in Mexico unless a bilateral agreement between the aviation
authorities covers it (NOM 4.10.16). The AFAC's form asks for a Mexican RFC tax ID and an official ID. The pages we read do not say how
a visitor without either registers.

## Where and how you can fly

The NOM sets rules for every class, and the limits below apply to recreational flying of a micro drone (up to 2 kg, NOM 5.1):

- **Height:** at most 122 m (400 ft) above the ground. Between 9.2 km and 18.5 km of the aerodromes in the NOM's list (Apéndice I), the limit is 100 m (328 ft).
- **Range:** no more than 457 m (1,500 ft) from the pilot.
- **Line of sight:** you must see the drone at all times, with no aid other than corrective lenses.
- **Aerodromes:** no flying within 9.2 km (5 NM) of any aerodrome on the SCT's current list (NOM 4.10.1). Helipads: 0.9 km (NOM 4.10.2).
- **Restricted areas:** not in prohibited, restricted or danger areas in the aeronautical information publication (AIP, ENR 5.1). Check NOTAMs before flying (NOM 4.10.5 and 4.10.6).
- **Time:** daylight only, between sunrise and sunset (NOM 4.10.10).
- **People:** do not fly over people unless they are involved in the flight or sheltered. Keep at least 10 m from anyone not involved. A drone of 250 g or less may fly over people.
- **Other:** one drone at a time (NOM 4.10.14), no dropping objects (4.10.3), no dangerous goods or weapons (4.10.7), no flying from a moving vehicle (4.10.12), and no flying under the influence (4.11.4).
- **Software:** a drone over 250 g must limit its own range and height (NOM 5.1.3).
- **Airspace:** give way to crewed aircraft (NOM 4.10.11).

For a small drone (over 2 kg to 25 kg) the NOM also requires that the pilot fly only within an authorised model-aircraft club (NOM 6.1.1 b),
must keep 30 m from anyone not involved for drones of 2 kg to 10 kg, and 50 m for drones of 10 kg to 25 kg (NOM 6.1.1 j). Visibility must
be at least 1.5 km, and flights must be in cloud-free weather (NOM 6.1.1 g and h). Drones over 25 kg need a certificate of registration (NOM 7.1.1 a).

## Age

No minimum age is set for flying. A minor's registration must be made by an adult (NOM 15.3, note 1).

## Penalties

The NOM sets no fines. It refers to the Civil Aviation Law, which we did not read.
