This guide follows the AAC's drone page and two AAC letters on drone registration and procedure. The letters are undated in the copies we
read; the file name of one says 2024. The AAC's live site did not respond from our network on 26 October 2026, so we read archived copies
from web.archive.org (July 2025). The texts are in Spanish and the translations are ours. We did not read Resolution 004-2021-NRA-DG-AAC,
which the letters cite: its archived copy did not load.

## Registration

The AAC's registration letter lists the documents: a copy of the operator's licence, proof of ownership, a copy of the ID, the equipment
specifications, the purchase invoice and a civil liability insurance policy. Registration happens in person at building 805, where the
form is filed in two copies and the drone gets its sticker. The letter also says a drone used for recreation must be registered, and that
its possessor must hold a licence.

## Flying

- **Special flights:** tell the AAC the purpose of the flight by email; it sends the special operations form. The AAC asks for WGS 84
  coordinates and heights in feet above mean sea level.
- **Panama City:** the Cinta Costera, Avenida Balboa and Calle 50 need a note to the Cinta Costera security service. Casco Antiguo needs a
  note to the police.
- **Restricted zones:** the AAC says the city is affected by airports, heliports and restricted zones, which the AIP lists. For DJI
  restricted areas, the letter names DJI's FlySafe contact and says that needs a licence and experience.
