This guide rests on excerpts of the Department of Aviation's pages, Decree No. 297 and Council of Ministers Resolution No. 972 (as
amended on 23 July 2025) that a web search returned on 26 October 2026. The texts are in Russian and the translations are ours.
**We did not open them ourselves.** A bill adding criminal and administrative penalties passed its first reading in July 2025; we
did not confirm it was adopted. Check the Department before you travel with a drone.
