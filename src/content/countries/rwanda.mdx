This guide follows the RCAA's drone pages (unmanned aircraft and UAS registration) and Part 27 of the Rwanda Civil Aviation
Regulations (RCAR Part 27, Unmanned Aircraft Systems), all read on 26 October 2026. Part 27 is in English, so there is no
translation. Check the RCAA for any change.

## Registration

Every drone operated in Rwanda must be registered (27.015). You apply by email, then register on the RCAA's Drone Portal once it replies.
The registration fee is set by the RCAA; the registration page refers to a separate drone charges document, which we did not open.

## Where and how you can fly

For ordinary flying, the standard conditions in 27.050 apply:

- **Height:** at or below 120 m (400 ft) above ground, by day.
- **Line of sight:** the drone must stay within the visual line of sight of the person operating it.
- **People:** no flying within 30 m of anyone not involved in the operation, or over a populated area.
- **Aerodromes:** not within 10 nautical miles (about 18.5 km) of the centre of an international controlled aerodrome, or
  5 nautical miles (about 9.3 km) of a domestic one. Conversion: 1 nautical mile is 1.852 km.
- **Areas:** not in a prohibited or restricted area, and not over a fire, police or emergency operation without approval.

Flights above 120 m, and flights in the radii above, fall under 27.090 and its further conditions, which we did not read in full.

## Arriving with a drone

A drone brought in from abroad, registered or not, must be declared at the airport or border on arrival (Appendix 1.2.1
and 1.2.4). The RCAA page says the Rwanda National Police; the regulation says airport or border security. The two names
differ and we could not settle which applies. Unregistered parts imported for a drone also need declaring (Appendix 1.2.2).
