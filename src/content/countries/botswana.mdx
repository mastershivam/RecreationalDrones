This guide rests on excerpts of the CAAB's registration page, its 2024 regulations and a 2019 flyer that a web search returned on
26 October 2026. **We did not open them ourselves.** The 2024 regulations took effect on 14 June 2024. Check the CAAB for any
change.

## Where you cannot fly

As summarised: a Botswana-registered drone may not cross an international border, and flying over prisons, police stations, and government or military sites, or over private property without permission, is restricted. The CAAB page lists a penalty of up to P5,000 and/or confiscation, where the 2019 flyer gave up to P50,000 and/or two years in prison.
