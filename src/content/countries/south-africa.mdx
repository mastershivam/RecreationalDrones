This guide follows SACAA's drones page, read on 20 October 2026. It shows only a "Copyright 2025" footer, and no date. The
regulation it cites, Part 101 of the Civil Aviation Regulations (in force since 1 July 2015), could not be read, because the
legislation site we tried listed only titles. Check SACAA for any change.

## Which rules apply

SACAA says private use means an individual's personal and private purposes with no commercial outcome, interest or gain, with
a Class 1A or 1B drone (under 7 kg and under 15 kJ of impact energy). Its table of approvals marks every approval (operator
certificate, letter of authority, pilot licence and certificate of registration) "N/A" for private operation. The page also
says Part 101 excludes model aircraft, which "operated at approved SAMAA airfields only", and that "operations as a hobbyist
are subject to the terms of Part 94".

## Registration

The registration section quotes Part 101.02.4(1): no unmanned aircraft may be operated in South Africa "unless such UAS has been
issued with a certificate of registration by the Director". **That conflicts with the table above, and the page does not say
which applies to private flying.** The application is on form CA-47R1, in the original, and the page says the new owner
supplies "the prescribed fee". The fee schedule we found is a PDF on another site, and we could not read a Part 101 figure in it.

## Where and how you can fly

For private flying, SACAA says:

- **Height:** do not fly higher than **150 ft** (about 45 m) from the ground, unless SACAA approves.
- **Aerodromes:** do not fly **10 km or closer** to an aerodrome (airport, helipad or airfield), unless SACAA approves.
- **People and property:** not **50 m or closer** to any person or group (sports fields, stadiums, schools, social events), a
  public road, or any property without the owner's permission.
- **Line of sight:** within sight at all times, and within 500 m of you and below the highest obstacle within 300 m. The page says
  private flying is "only in R-VLOS (day and night)", but its list of rules says to fly in daylight and clear weather.
- **Other limits:** not near manned aircraft, and not in controlled, restricted or prohibited airspace, or beside or over a
  nuclear power plant, prison, police station, court, national key point or strategic installation. Over 7 kg needs SACAA's
  approval.

## Not covered yet

- **Fees, penalties, insurance, minimum age and Remote ID:** none is on the page we read.
- **Visitors:** the page says nothing about flying a drone you bring into the country.
- **Part 101 and Part 94 themselves.** The guide rests on SACAA's summary, not the regulation text.
- **The 10 km circle on our map** is drawn from every airport's centre in OurAirports, so it shows more places than the
  rule covers. SACAA's own maps and the AIP show the real airspace.
