Romania applies the EU's common drone rules, set by the European Union Aviation Safety Agency (EASA). This
guide gives those rules, then links to Romania's own authority for what is national.

<EasaRules />

## In Romania

- **Insurance and penalties:** the Interior Ministry's guide on civil-registered drones (November 2025, our translation of
  its Romanian text) lists fines under Government Decision (H.G.) 859/2021, including one for flying without the insurance contract
  the decision requires. The text we extracted from the PDF is partly garbled, so we give no amounts; read them in the
  guide itself.
- **Restricted zones:** the same guide lists an authorisation from the Ministry of National Defence (MApN) for some
  restricted zones. We have not confirmed which zones or how it applies.
- **Register:** [Romania's registration page](https://rpas.caa.ro/). We could not reach it from our network when we checked.
- **Where you can fly:** [Romania's map of geographical zones](https://flightplan.romatsa.ro/init/drones).
- **Authority:** [Romanian Civil Aeronautical Authority](https://www.caa.ro/).

## Not covered yet

- **Romania's national rules.** AACR's own pages were blocked by a human-check page when we tried them, so we have not
  read them. What we say here comes from EASA and from the Interior Ministry's guide. Fees, how long registration lasts,
  the minimum age (a state may lower EASA's 16 to 12), and Remote ID for national drones are unverified. Check the
  authority above before you fly.
- **Where flying is banned.** Romania's geographical zones are on the map linked above, and we have not copied them.
  The Interior Ministry's guide appears to mention a 3,000 m distance from certified aerodromes, but its extracted text
  is too garbled to confirm, so we have not written it down.
