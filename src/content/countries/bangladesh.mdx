This guide rests on excerpts of a CAAB draft Air Navigation Order and its circular CAC-5 that a web search returned on 26 October
2026. **We did not open them ourselves**, and neither shows a date we could confirm. Check the CAAB for any change.

## Which rules apply

As summarised, a government policy, the Drone Registration and Flying Regulation 2020, set open, specific and certified categories. Open-category flights need no prior authorisation; educational, research and professional flights need a specific authorisation. The older circular CAC-5 asks for written permission 60 days ahead, bars flights within 10 nautical miles of an aerodrome and over open-air assemblies, and limits height to 200 ft AGL.
